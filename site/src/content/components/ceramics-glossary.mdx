## Presentation

`terms` is required. `title`, `intro`, and `alphabetNavigation` are optional. Every group requires a letter and repeated term records; each term requires a disclosure-style control and definition content, with optional media and related links.

The artwork demonstrates one static expanded relationship. Targets own terms, grouping, ordering, navigation, filtering, search, URL state, disclosure behavior, and result announcements. Alphabet controls do not prescribe whether they navigate, scroll, or filter.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep terms grouped and definitions available in a clear source order.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer filtering, scrolling, search, or URL behavior from the alphabet control selectors.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use alphabet controls only after the target chooses and implements their purpose.
- Keep related-term destinations target-owned.
- Do not remove the only accessible route to hidden terms when filtering.

## Accessibility

- Use semantic headings or description-list structures for terms and definitions.
- When terms collapse, synchronize `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and panel visibility.
- Give alphabet controls a clear purpose and current state.
- Announce filtering results when controls change the visible term set.
- Give informative term media useful alternative text.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** `.ceramics-glossary`, optional title, intro, alphabet navigation, and controls, required groups and letters, and repeated `.glossary-term` records with required trigger and content plus optional image, related region, and related links.

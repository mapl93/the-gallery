## Overview

Edition Badge is a passive, target-formatted edition statement with optional number, total, label, and limited-edition emphasis.

## Presentation

The default variant has no modifier. `limited` maps to `.edition-badge--limited`. Number, total, and label are optional because the target owns truthful formatting and may provide a complete statement through the composition.

The badge is passive. It does not verify provenance, scarcity, or inventory.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Format the complete edition statement so it reads coherently in the target locale.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer authenticity, availability, or inventory guarantees from the limited treatment.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use `limited` only when the target can support that designation.
- Keep numbering truthful and synchronized with the target record.
- Treat the separator as visual when it adds no spoken meaning.

## Accessibility

- Expose the edition statement as coherent readable text.
- Do not rely on limited-edition color alone.
- Keep decorative separators out of the accessible name.
- Avoid abbreviations that make the statement ambiguous when read aloud.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** `.edition-badge` with optional `.edition-badge__label`, number, separator, and total.

## Presentation

The selected main media, thumbnail selection, mobile dots, and desktop zoom are independent states. Media collections and index coordination remain consumer data.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Optimize product images for web — use WebP format and responsive srcset.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't overload product components with too many badges or CTAs — keep it clean.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Product components are designed to work together. The product page combines gallery, info, form, and related products.
</Callout>

- All images must have descriptive `alt` text.
- Thumbnail and dot controls should expose the selected media with `aria-selected` or an equivalent target-native state.
- Do not make zoom the only way to inspect product detail.

## Overview

FAQ Section provides an optional introduction around one required ordered Accordion composition. It owns section context and container-aware placement only; Accordion retains ownership of item anatomy, expansion, disabled state, keyboard activation, focus, trigger/panel relationships, and group policy.

The parent controls placement and optional introductory content. The required `items` slot contains one complete canonical Accordion rather than copying Accordion properties onto FAQ Section. When no title is supplied, use a generic `div.faq-section` instead of an unnamed native `section`; omit the entire component when no valid items are available.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Group concise questions around one subject and compose the canonical Accordion anatomy.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not hide information every user must read before completing a critical task or nest another Accordion inside an answer.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep answers direct. Link to a complete policy or guide when the answer becomes long, and choose FAQ only when disclosure meaningfully improves scanning.

## Accessibility

- Use a contextual visible heading and associate a native thematic section with that title. Use a generic root when the title is absent.
- Preserve canonical Accordion headings, native Button triggers, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, stable ids, native hidden panels, Enter/Space activation, visible focus, and disabled behavior.
- Do not add `role="region"` to every short answer. A target may opt in only when a panel is structurally significant and landmark count remains useful.
- Keep essential information visible outside collapsed answers and preserve meaningful question/answer source order without scripting.
- Group expansion policy is target-owned. Single, multiple, exact-exclusive, collapsible, and required-open modes are not FAQ Section properties.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use popovers for short supplemental content tied to a trigger.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't use popovers for blocking flows or complex forms. Use a modal or drawer instead.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="warning">
  Popovers are non-modal by default. They should close cleanly without trapping focus.
</Callout>

- Close on `Escape` key press.
- Keep `aria-expanded` synchronized on the trigger.
- Close on outside interaction.

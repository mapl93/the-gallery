## Presentation

The artwork uses documentation-owned trigger and close controls to exercise `open` through `aria-hidden="false"`, which canonical CSS recognizes as the visible state. Those controls are fixture behavior, not Mega Menu anatomy or public properties. `columns`, `promo`, and `featured` are independent optional slots; their records, order, destinations, media, and analytics remain target data.

The documented Default, Promo, and Featured compositions are supported arrangements, not contract variants. Mega Menu does not own the trigger, hover or click policy, keyboard entry, dismissal, mobile fallback, or mutual exclusion with other overlays.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Group destinations under clear headings and keep promotional regions subordinate to navigation.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not promote fixture columns, campaigns, or featured records into component defaults.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep columns scannable and avoid repeating the same destination across regions.
- Use native links for columns, promotional media, and featured destinations.
- Resolve interaction policy and mobile fallback in the consuming target.

## Accessibility

- Provide the required accessible navigation `label`.
- Keep `aria-hidden` synchronized with open presentation.
- Use logical headings within columns and mark the current destination with `aria-current` when applicable.
- Give promotional and featured links useful accessible names and meaningful image alternatives.
- The target runtime owns trigger relationships, keyboard entry, focus movement, `Escape` and outside dismissal, and focus restoration.

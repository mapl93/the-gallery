## Overview

Marquee presents an ordered line of target-owned text or content with an existing continuous-motion presentation and a static reduced-motion fallback.

The component pauses its track on pointer hover or focus within and becomes static under reduced motion. Content duplication, speed, direction, repetition, and runtime controls are intentionally not public properties.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use short, non-essential messages that remain understandable in the static fallback.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use continuous motion as the only place for critical policy, price, or status information.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Targets that duplicate items for seamless motion must treat those copies as presentation only.

## Accessibility

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and preserve a readable static layout.
- Hide animation-only duplicate content from assistive technology.
- Keep separators decorative and avoid unnecessary live-region announcements.
- Ensure any interactive content inside the track remains reachable and causes motion to pause while focused.

## Overview

Artist Statement presents a first-person artist narrative with a required artist
name and rich statement body. Portrait media, editorial label, highlighted
quotation and editorial signature are optional.

## Presentation

`name` and `body` are required. Portrait, editorial label, highlighted
quotation and signature are omitted completely when absent. The textual
narrative remains understandable without any image.

The layout responds to the component container. A portrait-present instance
stacks in narrow containers and uses a sticky one-third/two-thirds split only
when its own width permits it. A portrait-free instance always uses one readable
content column. Portrait and narrative source order never changes.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use the body for the artist's complete authored statement and reserve the quotation for a genuine excerpt.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use this component as an artist biography, authentication record, or proof that a mark is a verified signature.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use Artist Profile for biography, location, philosophy and destinations.
- Keep media source, intrinsic dimensions, responsive candidates, crop, focal
  point, loading and alternative purpose target-owned.
- Keep the visible artist name even when a visual signature or artist mark is
  present.
- Choose the artist-name heading level from the surrounding document.

## Accessibility

- Associate the native section with the contextual artist-name heading through
  a unique `aria-labelledby` and `id` pair.
- Use `blockquote` only for content genuinely quoted from the artist or another
  source; keep attribution outside the quotation.
- Give an informative portrait useful contextual alternative text. Use empty
  alt for decorative or redundant portrait/signature images.
- Do not replace a visible textual signature with a redundant ARIA label or
  claim signed/authenticated status without target evidence.
- Preserve portrait-before-narrative reading order at every width and in RTL.
- The neutral component is passive and introduces no focus stop, widget role,
  keyboard behavior, live region or motion.

## References

**Dependencies:** None. Artist Statement does not duplicate Artist Profile,
Card, Avatar, Button or Certificate behavior.

**Anatomy:** required `.artist-statement` root,
`.artist-statement__layout`, `.artist-statement__content`,
`.artist-statement__name`, and `.artist-statement__body`; conditional
`.artist-statement__layout--with-portrait`; optional
`.artist-statement__portrait` and image, `.artist-statement__eyebrow`,
`.artist-statement__quote`, `.artist-statement__signature`, and signature image.

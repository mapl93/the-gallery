## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use carousel when users need to browse a compact set of related slides.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't hide essential content only inside a carousel.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="warning">
  Auto-advancing carousels need pause controls and must respect reduced motion preferences.
</Callout>

- Give previous, next, and dot controls accessible names.
- Keep the active dot synchronized with `aria-current="true"`.
- Ensure slide content and controls are reachable by keyboard.
- All images must have descriptive `alt` text.

## Presentation

The primary artwork uses Drawer for the overlay, panel, title, close control, body, footer, modal naming, and focus lifecycle. The documentation fixture starts open; its close control and sibling trigger exercise only Drawer visibility, not a Cart Drawer commerce API.

`lineItems` is an optional canonical `.cart-lines > .cart-line` composition. Cart Line Item owns each record's media/identity/detail layout and composes Price, Quantity Selector, and target action Buttons. `summary` remains an optional target-formatted description-list region isolated from the later Cart Summary component. Both slots may be absent for target-authored empty content.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Compose canonical Cart Line Item records and keep their target data, quantities, prices, controls, and totals synchronized.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not reimplement line anatomy or calculate cart truth, mutation policy, or overlay lifecycle inside Cart Drawer presentation.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Preserve product context in every composed line action.
- Use the optional summary only when the target can update it after each confirmed cart mutation.
- Omit the summary when the target cart is empty; supply meaningful target-authored empty content.

## Accessibility

- Inherit Drawer dialog naming, focus containment, dismissal, scroll locking, and logical focus restoration.
- Inherit Cart Line Item list, destination, Price, Quantity Selector, Button, focus, and naming requirements.
- Announce mutation, error, item-count, and total results once according to the target's cart policy.
- After confirmed removal, place focus on a surviving line action or the Drawer title according to the surrounding target context.

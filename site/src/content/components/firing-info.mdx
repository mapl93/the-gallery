## Overview

Firing Info presents target-supplied technical terms and formatted values in a compact description-list surface.

## Presentation

The `details` slot is required and the title is optional. Every item pairs a term with a target-formatted value.

The component defines description-list anatomy only. Field inventory, units, qualifiers, values, ordering, and ceramics data records remain target-owned.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use familiar terms and include units or qualifiers directly in each value.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not treat the fixture fields as a required firing-data schema.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Include only details the target can source accurately.
- Keep terminology consistent within the containing product or editorial context.
- Order details for the reader rather than according to an assumed database shape.

## Accessibility

- Use description-list semantics for firing terms and values.
- Include units and qualifiers in readable text.
- Keep each term adjacent to its value in source order.
- Do not communicate firing conditions through visual styling alone.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** `.firing-info`, optional title, required `.firing-info__list`, and repeated `.firing-info__item` records containing `dt` and `dd` elements.

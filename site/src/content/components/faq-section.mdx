## Overview

FAQ Section provides an optional heading and supporting context around an ordered Accordion composition. Accordion retains ownership of expansion, keyboard, panel, and focus behavior.

The parent controls section placement and introductory content. The required `items` slot contains a complete Accordion composition rather than copying Accordion properties onto FAQ Section.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Group concise questions around one subject and compose the canonical Accordion anatomy.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not hide information users must read before they can complete a critical task.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep answer content direct and use links when an answer needs to point to a complete policy or guide.

## Accessibility

- Use a logical section heading and native buttons for Accordion triggers.
- Keep `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, panel IDs, and labelled regions synchronized through the Accordion implementation.
- Preserve Enter and Space activation and visible keyboard focus.
- Do not duplicate Accordion state or behavior at the FAQ Section root.

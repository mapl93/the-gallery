## Presentation

The artwork uses documentation-owned Button controls to exercise `open` through synchronized `aria-hidden` and `inert` state. The external trigger is connected with `aria-controls` and `aria-expanded`; trigger placement and activation policy remain target behavior rather than Mega Menu anatomy. `columns`, `promo`, and `featured` are independent optional slots whose records, order, destinations, media, and analytics remain target data.

The contract's Default, Promo, and Featured labels describe supported composition presets, not modifier classes or mutually exclusive semantic modes. Mega Menu does not own the trigger, hover or click policy, keyboard entry, dismissal, mobile fallback, or mutual exclusion with other overlays.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Group destinations under clear headings and keep promotional regions subordinate to navigation.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not promote fixture columns, campaigns, or featured records into component defaults.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep columns scannable and avoid repeating the same destination across regions.
- Use visible headings plus native lists and links for columns and featured destinations.
- Let visible destination text name promo/featured links; use empty image alternatives when media is decorative.
- Resolve interaction policy and mobile fallback in the consuming target.

## Accessibility

- Provide the required accessible navigation `label`.
- Keep `aria-hidden` and `inert` synchronized with open presentation.
- Use visible headings, `ul > li > a` grouping, and `aria-current="page"` for the actual current destination.
- Preserve ordinary Tab/Shift+Tab/Enter behavior; do not add `menu`, `menubar`, roving focus, arrow-key navigation, or a focus trap.
- Keep closed content out of focus and accessibility APIs. The target owns trigger activation, `Escape`/outside dismissal, focus restoration when moved, and mobile substitution.

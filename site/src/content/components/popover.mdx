## Overview

Popover reveals concise non-modal content in a floating surface anchored to an
explicit native Button trigger. It does not add a backdrop, inert the page,
lock scroll, or trap focus.

The trigger is the default visual anchor and stays available while the surface
is open. Position, side, collision handling, portal/top-layer choice and any
content-derived role remain target services rather than public v1 properties.
The decorative arrow is visible by default and can be omitted with
`showArrow={false}` without changing activation, positioning, dismissal, or
content semantics.

## Marketing use

A concise marketing explanation or small target-composed form may use Popover
when it remains anchored, non-modal and easy to dismiss. A campaign that must
block the page, demand a decision or contain a long workflow uses canonical
[Modal](/components/modal). The deprecated [Popup](/components/popup) identity
does not create a separate overlay component.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Popover for concise supplemental content or a small control group tied to a clearly labelled Button.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer dialog, menu or modal behavior from a floating visual surface.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="warning">
  Popover has no default ARIA role. Choose semantics from its actual content and use Modal when page interaction must be blocked.
</Callout>

- Keep `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` synchronized for class-driven
  targets, or use native `popovertarget` where appropriate.
- Close on `Escape`, outside interaction and repeated trigger activation.
- Preserve natural Tab order. If keyboard dismissal occurs after focus moved
  inside, restore focus to the trigger.
- Closed interactive content must be hidden or inert and unavailable to Tab.
- Capable Web targets may use `popover="auto"` and `:popover-open` for native
  top-layer and light-dismiss behavior.

## Visual Customization

Twenty-six public roles cover appearance and geometry. Minimum/maximum width start at 12.5rem/20rem and remain bounded by the viewport minus a total gutter of 2 times the responsive spacing base. These are content bounds, not a fixed width: intrinsic content selects the used width. Keep the minimum at or below the maximum; CSS minimum sizing wins if they conflict. An embedding target may add its available-container bound.

Block/inline padding factors start at 0.75/1, title-gap factor at 0.25, arrow inline-inset factor at 1. Surface and arrow share a 1px border token; the arrow's vertical inset derives from its size and that border. Arrow size starts at 0.5rem. Fixed rotation and physical top/left strokes keep the arrow pointing upward in LTR and RTL, while its inline placement follows direction. Large arrows/insets need contextual visual review; these tokens are not a collision engine.

Entrance displacement starts at 0.25rem and affects the closed-to-open translation; it does not control trigger distance. Title weight reuses semibold and shared typography includes both sizes and line heights. Open, Show arrow, native top-layer support, placement, dismissal and natural focus behavior remain independent. Studio/Exhibit place the surface in local flow to reserve its real height and compose complete Input anatomy; editable dimension values are example data, not Popover properties or defaults. Input styling comes from Input tokens.

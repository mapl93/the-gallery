## Overview

Passive metric display with target-formatted value, label, and optional directional change.

<Preview html={'<div class="stat"><span class="stat__value">1,234</span><span class="stat__label">Orders</span><span class="stat__change" data-direction="up">+12%</span></div>'} label="Stat / Statistic" />

## Presentation

Stat has one size and variant. Change Direction is semantic presentation on the optional change text; Neutral applies no directional color. Numeric value and change text use Inter through `--font-family-body`.

The same OpenType controls as Price are available independently: alternate digits (`ss01`), slashed zero (`zero`), tabular numbers (`tnum`), contextual alternates (`calt`), and fractions (`frac`). Unsupported features are ignored by the active font.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont><Do>Format numbers, currency, and percentages in the target locale before rendering.</Do><Dont>Do not infer whether an increase is good or bad from its mathematical sign alone.</Dont></DoDont>

Direction describes visual change, while the target decides the business meaning and wording.

## Accessibility

- Keep value, label, and change together in reading order.
- Include a sign or directional word so color is not the only cue.
- Use concise labels that remain meaningful outside a visual dashboard grid.

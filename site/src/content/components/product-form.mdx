## Presentation

Product Form owns one intrinsic vertical rhythm and a wrapping action row. Variant Selector, Quantity Selector, and Button keep their own anatomy, states, tokens, keyboard behavior, and value ownership.

The target may omit options for a product with no meaningful variants or omit Quantity when product policy fixes one. Product Form does not repeat Price: Product Info owns the one canonical price surface for this composition.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Resolve current options to one purchasable identifier and synchronize it before enabling or submitting the form.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not store a second mirrored object for child values or submit a stale variant identifier.</Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use the native form, named child controls, activated submitter, FormData, constraint validation, and reset lifecycle.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not add Ajax, Cart Drawer, inventory, analytics, or result-announcement policy to the target-neutral component.</Dont>
</DoDont>

### Target ownership

Neutral Web adds no Product Form runtime. Native submission is the required complete baseline. A capable target may intercept that same valid form and FormData to update a cart surface without navigation; it owns pending, errors, retry, focus, announcements, and rollback and must not create a parallel submission path. The shared product coordinator owns option-combination resolution, merchandise id, availability, quantity rules, and Price/media/inventory/URL synchronization.

Unavailable and pending are different states. Unavailable uses the native disabled submitter and a visible explanation when option content does not already provide one. Pending uses canonical Button busy semantics, keeps the label stable, and prevents repeat activation.

Accelerated checkout and a generic supplementary-field slot are excluded from neutral v1. Selling plans, personalization, uploads, gifts, and preorder flows require separately accepted canonical compositions. In Shopify, a merchant may show the canonical Quantity Selector or omit it when quantity is fixed to one.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Product Form provides the native submission boundary and feedback placement. Canonical children own their own names and validity; the target owns commerce result timing.
</Callout>

- Use a real `<form>` with an explicit `button[type="submit"]`.
- Give each submitted child control a stable name and preserve the activated submitter in FormData.
- Keep the resolved merchandise identifier synchronized and disable submission when no purchasable combination exists.
- Preserve native child validity, reset, keyboard behavior, labels, and accessible names.
- Keep visible, reading, and tab order aligned when the action row wraps or content localizes.
- Let the target choose feedback insertion, live-region role, focus movement, recovery, and removal so the page does not announce the same update more than once.

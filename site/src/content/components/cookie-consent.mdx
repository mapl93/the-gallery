## Overview

Cookie Consent presents a viewport-edge notice, Button-composed actions, and optional preference-layout anatomy without defining a consent policy or preference model.

The primary Exhibit artwork starts visible. Its sibling trigger is hidden while the banner is active, appears after dismissal, and can reopen the fixture. That open/dismiss/reopen behavior and bounded edge placement belong to the docs site; they do not define consent, persistence, or a component default.

`data-visible` controls banner presentation. `.cookie-preferences` and its repeated category anatomy provide layout only.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Define action meaning, storage, blocking, withdrawal, and jurisdiction-specific requirements in the consuming target before deployment.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not treat dismissal as consent or infer categories, required cookies, defaults, or control types from this fixture.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The preference model remains unresolved. This component does not decide which categories exist or how their choices are represented.

## Accessibility

- Use clear Button labels that describe each target-defined action.
- Keep the policy link keyboard reachable with visible focus.
- Do not equate closing the banner with consent unless an accepted target policy explicitly defines that meaning.
- If preferences open in a modal surface, that surface owns naming, focus containment, `Escape`, and focus restoration.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences for banner transitions.

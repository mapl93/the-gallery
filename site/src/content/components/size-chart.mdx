## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Product components are designed to work together. The product page combines gallery, info, form, and related products.
</Callout>

- Ensure proper color contrast ratios (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text) when customizing tokens.
- Use dialog semantics and focus management for the open chart.
- Keep unit toggle state synchronized with `aria-pressed` or native pressed state.
- Preserve table semantics for measurement matrices.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use these specialty components to tell the artisan story behind your products.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't use generic product descriptions — ceramics customers value process, materials, and provenance.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Ceramics components are designed specifically for artisan e-commerce. They showcase craft and materials.
</Callout>

- Ensure proper color contrast ratios (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text) when customizing tokens.

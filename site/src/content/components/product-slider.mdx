## Presentation

The native horizontal track owns scrolling and snap behavior. Product collections remain consumer data, while optional navigation controls provide a non-drag entry point.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Optimize product images for web — use WebP format and responsive srcset.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't overload product components with too many badges or CTAs — keep it clean.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Product components are designed to work together. The product page combines gallery, info, form, and related products.
</Callout>

- Expose previous and next controls with clear accessible names.
- Keep every Product Card reachable by keyboard and touch.
- Do not rely only on drag gestures to reveal additional products.

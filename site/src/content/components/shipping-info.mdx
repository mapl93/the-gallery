## Overview

Shipping Info presents a passive native list of target-owned service or policy facts. Every item has a visible label; supporting text and a decorative visual are optional.

The required `items` slot owns one or more source-ordered native list items. Every valid item has a non-empty visible label; supporting text and a decorative visual are optional. The contract does not define a fixed icon library or structured service schema.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use short, verifiable service facts that help users understand fulfillment or policy expectations.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not present promotional claims that the target cannot fulfill.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep each label and supporting statement understandable without its icon.

Use links inside target-owned item content when a complete policy is useful. Do not make the passive item wrapper itself clickable or focusable.

## Accessibility

- Use native `ul` / `li` markup. `role="list"` preserves list exposure in environments where the shared reset suppresses visual markers.
- Keep the complete fact in visible text and do not rely on an icon, image, position, or color.
- Hide optional visuals from assistive technology; unique visual-only meaning is outside the S14 contract.
- Use native links when an item leads to a complete shipping, return, or security policy.
- Maintain sufficient text contrast, preserve source order, and omit the root when no valid labelled item remains.

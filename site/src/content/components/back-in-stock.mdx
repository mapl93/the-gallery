## Overview

Back in Stock is an unavailable-product notification profile. It provides a concise product context and one native form composed from canonical Input and Button; the consuming target owns the requested product or variant, provider, consent, request, and confirmed result.

## Presentation

D8 owns the framed context, heading, supporting copy, intrinsic form layout, and the `idle`, `submitting`, `confirmed`, and `retryableError` request presentations. The email field is the complete canonical Input, including its visible label, associated message, passive icon, native value, and Default/Error/Success/Warning validation families. The submitter is the canonical primary Button, including disabled, focus, busy, and reduced-motion behavior.

At wider component widths the field and action share one row. The component stacks them from its own inline capacity, not from the browser viewport. Validation messages may change independently without turning a valid email into a confirmed provider registration. A target drives request state only after binding the form to the exact selected variant and current market.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>State exactly which availability notification the customer is requesting and wait for the provider response before confirming it.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not equate a native submit event with success or reuse the address for unrelated marketing without the required consent.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Render the profile only when the target has an unambiguous selected variant, current market and valid notification service.
- Use `type="email"`, a stable target field name, `autocomplete="email"`, a visible label, and native `required` when the email is mandatory.
- Supply the exact selected variant and current market through target-owned form/model data; the class contract does not standardize provider field names.
- Set `requestState=submitting` only while registration is in flight, `confirmed` only after provider acceptance, and `retryableError` when the customer may correct or retry.
- Keep field validation separate from provider feedback. A syntactically valid email is not proof that a request was stored.
- Keep the form present. Submitting and confirmed make its email read-only and prevent resubmission; retryable error keeps it editable and available.
- Treat availability-only consent separately from marketing consent. The integration owns notice, verification, duplicate, abuse, retention, expiry, delivery and unsubscribe policy.

## Accessibility

- Name the native form from the visible heading and keep the heading rank contextual to its host page.
- Preserve canonical Input label/message relationships and use `aria-invalid="true"` only for an error.
- Preserve Input-then-Button source, reading, and keyboard order when the container stacks or changes direction.
- Use canonical Button busy semantics during a real request; keep its visible label and block repeat activation. Confirmed also blocks resubmission without removing the focused context.
- Insert or update truthful form-level feedback only when a target result exists. Use status semantics for non-blocking confirmed results and appropriate alert, summary, or focus treatment for blocking errors.
- Do not pre-render a hidden success message, announce successful registration before provider confirmation, or treat the notification request as marketing consent.

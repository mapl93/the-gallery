## Presentation

The summary remains a compact text button and the location list appears directly below it. Status icons reinforce readable availability text, while the chevron rotates to communicate disclosure state.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Pair each location with a plain-language availability statement and realistic readiness estimate.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not imply availability from color or an icon alone.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Order locations by usefulness to the customer, such as proximity or availability.
- Keep hours and readiness details subordinate to the location name.
- Collapse long location lists when the surrounding product page is dense.

## Accessibility

- Keep the button's `aria-expanded` value synchronized with both the root state and visible content.
- Connect the disclosure button to its content with `aria-controls`.
- Pair availability icons with readable status text.
- Preserve a visible 4px focus indicator around the complete trigger.

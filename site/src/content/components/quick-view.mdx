## Overview

Quick View composes Modal, Product Gallery, and an optional reduced Product Form into a focused product-purchase surface. It may also provide a direct link to the complete product page.

## Presentation

Quick View uses the ADR 0069 overlay family: the trigger and surface are siblings, the modal is visible on first load, and the trigger does not constrain its geometry. Modal owns backdrop visibility, focus management, dismissal, and labelled-dialog behavior.

`gallery` and `title` are required. `vendor`, `description`, the reduced `productForm`, and the complete-product link are optional. Quick View does not redefine the behavior of Product Gallery or Product Form.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Quick View for a reduced purchase decision that can be completed without the full product page.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not duplicate every product-page section inside the modal.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Include only the media, information, and controls needed for the immediate decision.
- Provide `fullLinkLabel` and `href` when important details remain on the product page.
- Keep selected variant and availability state synchronized with the commerce target.

## Accessibility

- Inherit Modal focus trapping, Escape dismissal, backdrop behavior, and focus return.
- Use `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and a label relationship to the product title.
- Keep gallery alternatives, variant labels, and purchase feedback available in the modal context.
- Include a visible close mechanism in addition to supported dismissal behavior.
- Preserve a descriptive focus indicator on the full-details link.

## Overview

Exhibition Page composes a media-optional exhibition hero, information and metadata,
featured works, and participating artists in a preserved source order. Its layout
responds to the component container rather than the browser viewport.

## Presentation

`title` is required. Hero media, label, dates, location, narrative description,
metadata details, featured works, and participating artists are optional
target-owned content. Without hero media, the hero uses the statement surface and
primary text; with media, the target applies `.exhibition-hero--with-media`, the
media wrapper, decorative scrim, and inverse text.

The composition preserves hero, information, works, and artist reading order as
its information and record layouts respond to available component width. The
featured-work and participating-artist slots intentionally use passive fixture
records in the artwork. ADR 0080 does not decide whether Product Card, Artist Card,
links, or another dependency owns those records or interactions.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep the source order stable, omit absent optional regions, and let each target supply exhibition records and media semantics.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not assign Product Card, Artist Card, activation, filtering, commerce, or CMS ownership to the open composition slots.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use the hero for exhibition identity, dates, and location rather than unrelated promotion.
- Present repeated exhibition metadata as native name-value pairs.
- Use native lists for repeated passive records; target-owned links and controls retain their own semantics and focus.
- Keep works and participating artists target-owned until their dependency and interaction ownership is accepted.
- Do not expose hero height, crop, columns, density, scrim, or spacing as semantic properties.

## Accessibility

- Label the embedded article from the exhibition title. A full target page owns its outer `main` and contextual page-heading level.
- Use visible-heading sections for works and artists. Keep untitled information neutral unless the target supplies a localized name.
- Give informative hero, work, and artist media useful alternative text; use empty alt for decorative or nearby-redundant images.
- Use native `dl`, `ul`, `li`, `figure`, and machine-readable start/end `time` markup when the target has structured values.
- Passive records add no focus stops. Target-owned links and controls retain their own keyboard, focus, name, and activation requirements.
- Preserve source reading order at every component width and writing direction.

## References

**Dependencies:** None. Product Card and Artist Card are not dependencies while
record and interaction ownership remains unresolved.

**Anatomy:** required `.exhibition-page`, `.exhibition-hero`,
`.exhibition-hero__content`, and `.exhibition-hero__title`; optional hero media,
image, overlay, label, dates, and location; optional neutral `.exhibition-info`
description and semantic metadata anatomy; optional visible-heading
`.exhibition-works` and `.exhibition-artists` compositions; optional compact artist
item, avatar, content, name, and role.

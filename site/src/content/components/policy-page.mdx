## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep page templates simple and focused on their primary purpose.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't add navigation-heavy sidebars to simple pages like 404 or policy pages.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Page components map to Shopify template types. Each has a corresponding .json template file.
</Callout>

- Ensure proper color contrast ratios (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text) when customizing tokens.

## Overview

Tooltip supplies short, non-essential context for one focusable trigger. Its
explicit content describes the trigger without replacing the trigger's own name.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Tooltip for a brief definition, icon explanation, or optional keyboard hint.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't hide required instructions, validation, actions, links, or touch-essential information in Tooltip.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The trigger may compose Button, Link, or another focusable consumer element.
Tooltip owns only the explicit content presentation. Targets own ids, Escape
dismissal, optional delay, placement collision, portals, and open-state events.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="warning">
  Tooltip content supplements a trigger through `aria-describedby`; it must not
  become the trigger's `aria-label`.
</Callout>

- Give the trigger its own visible and accessible name.
- Link the trigger to one `role="tooltip"` node with a stable unique id.
- Reveal on hover and focus, keep the content hoverable and persistent, and let
  Escape dismiss it without moving focus.
- Keep content short and non-interactive. Use Popover for links, buttons, or rich
  structure, and visible helper text for required information.
- Do not depend on hover-only access on touch devices.

## Visual Customization

Six geometry tokens complement the ten shared roles. Maximum width starts at 18rem, arrow depth at 5px, trigger gap factor at 0.5, block/inline padding factors at 0.25/0.625 and total viewport gutter factor at 2. Factors multiply the existing responsive spacing base; they do not require duplicate size catalogues. The CSS triangle base remains twice its depth, and its color follows the surface.

The invisible pointer bridge derives its height from the same trigger-gap token so the user can cross the configured space into the content. Centering uses the same physical axis as its translation, fixing the former RTL displacement without a configurable direction token. Semantic direction still comes from the consumer.

Studio reserves the measured content height plus the token-driven gap, keeping the tooltip above its trigger within the demonstration area. That private measurement follows text and type changes and is not a public token or placement service.

The viewport-width bound does not implement collision-aware positioning near an edge. Targets still own delay, collision, portal and dismissal services. Hover/focus, explicit non-interactive tooltip content and its aria-describedby relationship remain required. Typography, color, radius and motion reuse the existing shared inputs; forced colors and reduced motion retain their system treatments.

## Overview

Author Card presents author identity, an optional Avatar, optional role and biography text, and target-owned profile or social links. It supports full and compact presentations.

`full` is the default and has no modifier class. `compact` maps to `.author-card--compact`, removes the frame treatment, and visually hides the biography and links. The Avatar slot controls its own size.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use full presentation when biography and links provide useful article context.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not treat author identity, destinations, or social-provider choices as component defaults.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Use compact presentation for a short byline composition. The target supplies author data and chooses whether profile or social destinations are available.

## Accessibility

- Use a heading or text element for the author name that fits the surrounding article hierarchy.
- Avoid repeating an adjacent visible author name in Avatar alternative text unless the image adds useful information.
- Give icon-only profile or social links specific accessible names.
- Use links for destinations and buttons only for actions.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus for all composed links and controls.

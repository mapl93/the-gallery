## Overview

Wishlist is a heading-labelled, target-controlled exact-variant saved-item
collection. It arranges canonical Product Cards in a native list and may
replace a resolved empty collection with canonical Empty State. The accepted
hybrid profile combines anonymous local-device and authenticated account
records, but persistence, reconciliation, loading, mutation, focus recovery,
and announcements remain target-owned.

## Presentation

The root is a native `section` labelled by its required visible title. `count`
is optional complete target-formatted text and is deliberately not a live
region. The populated collection is `ul.wishlist__grid`; each
`li.wishlist__item` contains one complete `article.card.product-card`.

`products` remains one target-controlled slot rather than a flattened array of
Wishlist product props. It is optional so a resolved-empty target can omit the
list and supply `emptyState`; once the lifecycle is resolved, one of those two
compositions is required. Product identity, media, links, vendor, price,
availability, ordering, and contextual commands stay inside Product Card or
the target data composition.

Every saved record represents one exact variant or merchandise selection. The
target uses stable variant identity for merge/deduplication while retaining the
parent product identity for navigation. Multiple variants of the same product
may coexist, so Product Card must visibly name each option selection and the
contextual Remove command must include both product and selection in its
accessible name. Image, swatch, or color alone is insufficient.

The runtime fixture places a canonical small outline Button in Product Card's
footer. On a saved-items page, `Remove` is a command and has no `aria-pressed`.
A persistent Save toggle that remains visible in saved and unsaved states is a
different potential component and is not silently defined by U8.

Sold-out or no-longer-available exact variants remain in the collection with
truthful text, parent-product navigation, and explicit Remove. The target never
silently deletes them or converts them into product-level favorites.

### Hybrid persistence and sign-in merge

Anonymous local records and authenticated account records are united
idempotently after sign-in using exact variant identity. Neither source
destructively replaces the other. The authenticated service returns the
authoritative merged result, and the target clears local records only after
confirmed success. Failure preserves local truth for retry. Neutral Wishlist
stores nothing and exposes no provider, account, or storage API.

### Resolved empty composition

`emptyState` is optional and is used only after the target has resolved that the
saved collection is empty. The target chooses contextual heading rank, copy,
action, focus, and announcement behavior. Loading and errors must not be
presented as an empty collection.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Project exact-variant records and count from one authoritative target result.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not store saved IDs, query products, or duplicate Product Card inside Wishlist.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use one native list item per complete canonical Product Card.
- Preserve Product Card's media ratio, title link, Price, Button, focus, and
  reduced-motion behavior; Wishlist only arranges the collection.
- Use a Button for removal or mutation and a Link for navigation.
- Update list/count/empty truth only after confirmed mutation success. Preserve
  the record and explain failure when confirmation does not arrive.
- Decide a logical focus destination when a successful removal deletes the
  focused card: a surviving adjacent action, collection title, or empty-state
  action may be appropriate depending on the resulting context.
- Union local and account records idempotently by exact variant identity after
  sign-in; clear local records only after confirmed account success.
- Treat guest storage, customer accounts, cross-device sync, consent, retention,
  limits, privacy, and authorization as target policy.

## Accessibility

- Give the visible title a unique ID and reference it through
  `section[aria-labelledby]`. Do not add application, grid, selection, or
  listbox roles.
- Keep the product collection as `ul > li`; Product Card keeps its own `article`
  and heading/link semantics.
- Give every removal command a product-and-saved-option-specific accessible
  name. A command Button has no `aria-pressed`.
- If a future persistent Save toggle is accepted, keep its label stable while
  `aria-pressed` communicates state and expose controlled/uncontrolled ownership
  at that component.
- Keep count passive. Target-owned Status or Alert output announces pending,
  success, failure, undo, resulting count, and empty transitions when needed.
- Do not leave focus on a removed node, move focus merely to announce a status,
  or display Empty State while loading/error truth is unresolved.
- Keep every exact option selection visible in text and retain unavailable
  records with a truthful localized status and explicit user choices.
- Protect customer-specific saved data according to the selected account,
  guest-storage, privacy, consent, authorization, and retention model.

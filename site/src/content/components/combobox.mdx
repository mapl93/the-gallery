## Overview

Searchable text field with a filterable listbox for autocomplete and richer option matching.

## Presentation

Combobox supports `default`, `error`, `success`, and `warning` validation families independently of its open state.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Combobox when users benefit from typing, filtering, autocomplete, or remote results.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't replace a short finite Select with a more complex searchable field.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

- Associate the visible label with the input.
- Keep `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and `aria-activedescendant` synchronized.
- Use `role="listbox"` and `role="option"` for the popup and options.
- Support Arrow keys, Home, End, Enter, and Escape without moving DOM focus from the input.

## Overview

Article Hero introduces an article with a required title and optional category, media, and target-formatted metadata. It supports full-image, split, and text-only editorial presentations.

`full` maps to `.article-hero--image`, `split` maps to `.article-hero--split`, and `text-only` maps to `.article-hero--text`. Full media uses `.article-hero__bg` with an optional `.article-hero__overlay`; split media uses `.article-hero__media`.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Choose the presentation from the article context and available media.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not require media for the text-only presentation or treat fixture metadata as a component default.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The target supplies full or split media and formats publication, author, and reading metadata. Use the full-image presentation only when text remains legible over the complete responsive crop.

## Accessibility

- Use a page-appropriate heading level for the article title, normally the page `h1`.
- Give informative media useful alternative text and decorative media an empty `alt` value.
- Verify overlaid title, category, and metadata contrast across every image crop, theme, and viewport.
- Keep metadata such as publication dates in semantic elements, including `time` with a machine-readable `datetime` value.

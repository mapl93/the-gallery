## Overview

Popover reveals concise non-modal content in a floating surface anchored to an
explicit native Button trigger. It does not add a backdrop, inert the page,
lock scroll, or trap focus.

The trigger is the default visual anchor and stays available while the surface
is open. Position, side, collision handling, portal/top-layer choice and any
content-derived role remain target services rather than public v1 properties.

## Marketing use

A concise marketing explanation or small target-composed form may use Popover
when it remains anchored, non-modal and easy to dismiss. A campaign that must
block the page, demand a decision or contain a long workflow uses canonical
[Modal](/components/modal). The deprecated [Popup](/components/popup) identity
does not create a separate overlay component.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Popover for concise supplemental content or a small control group tied to a clearly labelled Button.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer dialog, menu or modal behavior from a floating visual surface.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="warning">
  Popover has no default ARIA role. Choose semantics from its actual content and use Modal when page interaction must be blocked.
</Callout>

- Keep `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` synchronized for class-driven
  targets, or use native `popovertarget` where appropriate.
- Close on `Escape`, outside interaction and repeated trigger activation.
- Preserve natural Tab order. If keyboard dismissal occurs after focus moved
  inside, restore focus to the trigger.
- Closed interactive content must be hidden or inert and unavailable to Tab.
- Capable Web targets may use `popover="auto"` and `:popover-open` for native
  top-layer and light-dismiss behavior.

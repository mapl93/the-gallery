## Presentation

`steps` is required. `eyebrow`, `heading`, `intro`, and the separate `media` slot are optional. Each step requires a title and can include target-formatted numbering, description, and media.

The artwork stays editorial and omits the optional video surface. Targets own all records and destinations plus provider choice, consent, loading, playback, poster behavior, captions, modal presentation, and analytics.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep tour steps useful and understandable in their source order.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not add autoplay, provider embeds, playback actions, or modal behavior without a target-owned media policy.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use step media to clarify the place or process described by the text.
- Keep visible numbering consistent with the source order.
- Add the media slot only when the target supplies an accessible playback experience.

## Accessibility

- Preserve tour steps in logical source order.
- Provide alternative text for informative studio imagery.
- If a target supplies video, provide native or equivalent controls and captions where needed.
- Give playback actions an accessible name.
- Do not autoplay or load third-party media by contract assumption.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** `.studio-tour`, optional header, eyebrow, heading, and intro, required steps with repeated step records, optional step media, number, and description, required step title, and optional video region, poster, and play surface.

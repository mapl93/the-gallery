## Overview

Cart Line Item represents one product in the cart with required media, product identity, destination, and formatted line price. Variant information, quantity, compare price, and secondary actions are optional.

## Presentation

The required anatomy is media, product information, title link, and current line price. The optional Quantity Selector is composed inside `.cart-line__quantity`; Cart Line Item does not redefine its native stepping, bounds, or validation behavior.

The target formats `price` and `comparePrice`. Remove and save-for-later are independent optional actions and remain visually secondary to product navigation and quantity editing.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Update the line price and cart summary immediately after a valid quantity change.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not format commerce amounts or inventory rules inside the presentation layer.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Include variant text only when it distinguishes the selected purchasable item.
- Preserve product context in remove and save-for-later labels.
- Disable quantity transitions that exceed target-owned inventory boundaries.

## Accessibility

- Provide useful alternative text through `imageAlt`; use empty alternative text only when adjacent content makes the image redundant.
- Keep `title` as a descriptive product link to `href`.
- Give remove and save actions accessible names that include the product title.
- Provide an accessible name for the Quantity Selector and announce resulting total or availability changes.
- Keep every interactive element keyboard reachable with a visible focus indicator.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Display aggregate ratings prominently — social proof drives conversions.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't hide negative reviews — authenticity builds trust.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Review components work with Shopify product metafields or third-party review apps.
</Callout>

- Ensure proper color contrast ratios (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text) when customizing tokens.

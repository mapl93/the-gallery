## Overview

Hero Section presents a page-level message with a required heading and optional media, contrast overlay, eyebrow, supporting copy, Button-composed actions, or target-owned slides.

`full` is the default media-backed presentation and has no modifier class. `split` maps to `.hero-section--split`, `text-only` to `.hero-section--text-only`, `parallax` to `.hero-section--parallax`, `slideshow` to `.hero-section--slideshow`, and `fullscreen` to `.hero-section--fullscreen`. Parallax and slideshow are presentation hooks only: scroll behavior, navigation, timing, announcements, and playback remain target-owned.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use one clear heading and a restrained action hierarchy that supports the page purpose.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not imply slideshow navigation or parallax behavior unless the target provides the complete interaction.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep media, overlay, and foreground contrast coordinated. Treat slide records as ordered target content rather than a source for invented carousel settings.

## Accessibility

- Choose a heading level that fits the surrounding page hierarchy.
- Give informative media useful alternative text and use an empty alternative for decorative media.
- Keep links as links and commands as native buttons through the composed Button contract.
- When a target enables slideshow or parallax behavior, it must define focus order, announcements, pause controls, reduced-motion behavior, and current-slide state.

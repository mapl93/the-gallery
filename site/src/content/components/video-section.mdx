## Overview

Video Section frames target-owned poster, video, embed, or player media with an optional native play-intent action and visible caption. It does not prescribe playback behavior.

`contained` is the default padded presentation. `fullwidth` maps to `.video-section--fullwidth`. The optional button expresses play intent only; media loading, playback state, controls, captions, errors, and external-player APIs remain target-owned.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Provide a representative poster and let the target supply a complete accessible media experience.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not treat the visual play control as evidence that playback, autoplay, or modal behavior exists.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Use the visible caption for context, not as a replacement for media captions or a transcript.

## Accessibility

- Give the play-intent button a contextual accessible name.
- Provide captions and a transcript for meaningful spoken or auditory content.
- Respect autoplay restrictions and reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep native player controls keyboard accessible and expose loading, error, and playback state through the target implementation.

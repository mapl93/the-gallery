## Presentation

The required `steps` slot supplies ordered step records. The section title, visible target-formatted step number, and step media are optional; every step requires a name and explanation.

Wide layouts may alternate media visually. Source order remains the reading order and stacks without reversal on narrow viewports.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Break a technique into steps that remain understandable in source order without relying on the layout.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use visual alternation to imply a different sequence or promote fixture steps into a fixed process schema.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep step names action-oriented and descriptions concise.
- Add media when it clarifies a process detail.
- Let the target own process records, numbering, and editorial depth.

## Accessibility

- Preserve technique steps in logical source order.
- Use headings or ordered-list semantics appropriate to the containing document.
- Describe informative process media and hide decorative media from assistive technology.
- Do not rely on large decorative numbers as the only expression of sequence.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** `.technique-explainer`, optional header and title, and repeated `.technique-step` records with optional number, required name and description, and optional media.

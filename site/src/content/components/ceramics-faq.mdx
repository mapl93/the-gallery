## Presentation

`items` is required. `heading`, `subheading`, and `contact` are optional. Every item requires a visible question and answer projected through canonical Accordion anatomy.

The artwork starts with one answer expanded so both states remain reviewable. Targets own records, ordering, stable ids, heading rank, initial expansion, single-or-multiple group policy, answer accuracy, localization, rich-content trust, and real destinations.

Category controls are intentionally absent from the v1 API. Navigation and in-place filtering require different URL, focus, result, empty, and announcement lifecycles; selecting one remains an open product decision rather than an inert visual control.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Compose Accordion and Link instead of duplicating their markup, focus, visibility, motion, or navigation behavior.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not hide essential safety or universally required care information only inside optional disclosures.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep questions concise, answers source-backed, and records in meaningful source order.
- Use a real descriptive follow-up destination; do not render placeholder or prevented Links.
- Consider visible headings or separate pages when user evidence does not support disclosure interaction.
- Add categories only after one complete navigation or filtering mode is explicitly accepted.

## Accessibility

- Fit the optional section heading and Accordion trigger headings into the surrounding document hierarchy.
- Synchronize trigger ids, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, panel ids, optional region labels, and native `hidden` state.
- Preserve native Button click, Enter, Space, focus retention, and normal Tab order.
- Avoid region landmark proliferation when a target can expose many panels at once.
- Keep essential safety, legal, eligibility, and universally required care information visible outside optional answers.
- Verify long and localized questions and answers at narrow widths, 200 percent zoom, and right-to-left direction.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** Accordion, Link.

**Anatomy:** `.ceramics-faq`, optional header, heading and subheading, required `.ceramics-faq__list.accordion`, repeated `.accordion__item.ceramics-faq__item` with canonical heading/trigger/panel/content, and optional contact text plus canonical Link.

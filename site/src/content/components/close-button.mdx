Compact icon-only button that requests dismissal of a dialog, drawer, toast, alert, or another owned surface.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Name the surface being dismissed when multiple close actions may be present.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use Close Button for destructive deletion or backward navigation.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The consumer owns the state update, exit animation, focus restoration, and removal of the dismissed surface.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Click, Enter, and Space are native button behavior. Escape belongs to the containing overlay or dismissible surface.
</Callout>

- Use `type="button"` inside forms.
- Provide a contextual name such as `Close dialog` or `Dismiss alert`.
- Hide the decorative icon from assistive technology.
- Return focus according to the containing component's dismissal contract.
- Do not attach Escape handling to the button itself.
- Decorative color and background transitions are removed when reduced motion is requested.

## Visual Customization

Studio and Exhibit expose all thirteen public visual roles. `--space-close-button-size` starts at 32px and `--space-close-button-icon-size` at 20px; both are independently editable. Resting fill starts transparent. Focus width/offset, shared state colors, radius, motion and disabled opacity also remain token-driven. The control stays borderless and centers its icon from those two dimensions.

Re-evaluate target size, icon fit and focus visibility in the containing surface after customization. The container still owns dismissal, Escape and focus restoration.

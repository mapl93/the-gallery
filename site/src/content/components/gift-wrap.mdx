## Overview

Gift Wrap Option composes a native Checkbox with a target-formatted optional price. Selection changes target cart state; the component does not define the sellable gift-wrap item or pricing rule.

## Presentation

`label` is required and supplies the visible accessible option name. `selected` and `disabled` default to `false` and map to the composed native Checkbox. `price` is optional and remains target-formatted.

Gift Wrap Option owns the cart-specific label and price arrangement. Checkbox retains native selection, disabled, keyboard, and form semantics.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Synchronize selection and price with the target's gift-wrap cart state.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not imply that gift wrapping is free or available when the target says otherwise.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the option independent unless the target defines additional required choices.
- Update cart totals after selection changes.
- Explain unavailability in surrounding content when `disabled` is active.

## Accessibility

- Keep the native checkbox and visible `label` inside the same wrapping label.
- Preserve the native selected and disabled states on `.checkbox__input`.
- Include the price in nearby readable content without making it the checkbox's only name.
- Announce resulting cart-total changes after selection.
- Do not rely on the check indicator or price color alone to communicate state.

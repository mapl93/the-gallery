## Presentation

The header combines required `title` with optional target-owned `meta`. The example date is fixture content inside that slot.

`tracking` contains repeated steps and their current-state semantics. The labels and number of steps in the artwork are illustrative; the contract does not define a fixed tracking sequence. `lineItems` composes Cart Line Item anatomy for purchased products without quantity, remove, or save-for-later controls.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Map metadata, tracking, and line items from the same authenticated order record.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not expose cart mutation controls on purchased line items.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Let the target determine which tracking steps exist and which step is current.
- Format order identifiers, dates, statuses, and prices in the target layer.
- Include only order metadata that helps the customer understand fulfillment or payment.
- Keep purchased line items linked to useful product detail when that destination remains available.

## Accessibility

- Protect order details behind the target authentication system and authorize access to the requested order.
- Mark the active tracking step with `aria-current="step"` and readable text.
- Keep tracking dots decorative; do not rely on their color to communicate state.
- Label the purchased-items region and preserve a logical reading order.
- Remove cart-only mutation controls from composed purchased line items.

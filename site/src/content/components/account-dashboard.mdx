## Presentation

The header combines the required `greeting` with the optional `headerActions` slot. The example action is fixture content; authentication and navigation behavior come from the target.

`cards` is one required slot. Card titles, descriptions, icons, destinations, count, and order remain repeated fixture or target data rather than top-level Dashboard properties. The canonical grid adapts from one to two and three columns at its existing breakpoints.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use cards for clear destinations into distinct account areas.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not expose sensitive customer or order details merely to fill the overview.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Derive the greeting and available destinations from authenticated target data.
- Keep each card focused on one destination with descriptive link text.
- Omit unavailable destinations instead of rendering empty cards.
- Keep account-level actions separate from the card collection.

## Accessibility

- Protect the dashboard route through the target authentication system.
- Use a logical heading hierarchy for the greeting and card titles.
- Give every card destination clear link text rather than relying on the surrounding card.
- Keep decorative card icons hidden from assistive technology.
- Do not expose sensitive data in summary copy without clear user intent.

## References

### Composition

The card collection is target-owned slot content. The repeated card data in the artwork is a fixture, not a canonical property schema for individual cards.

## Presentation

`heading` and `steps` are required. `eyebrow`, `intro`, and supplementary `media` are optional. Invalid required composition is omitted instead of rendering an unnamed or empty section.

The ordered lane, steps, numbering, optional step images and descriptions, focus treatment, intrinsic overflow, RTL and forced-color behavior come from canonical Process Timeline. Studio Tour adds only the contextual introduction and supplementary-media placement.

The review fixture keeps supplementary media passive. Targets may replace it with native video or a complete target-native player only after they own media type, provider, privacy/consent, loading, controls, captions, transcripts, playback, modal presentation and analytics.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep the tour useful in source order and use the canonical Process Timeline composition.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not recreate step layout or add poster-like playback controls without a complete target-owned player.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep every step concise and parallel: required title, optional informative image, and optional description.
- Use the supplementary slot only for self-contained media that adds context beyond an individual step.
- Keep heading rank, records, localization, media assets and alternative text target-owned.
- Do not expose columns, orientation, density, marker style, connectors or media ratio as Studio Tour properties.

## Accessibility

- Render only with a non-empty visible heading and at least one direct ordered-list item.
- Associate the section and canonical lane with the same target-unique heading id.
- Preserve native `ol`/`li` order and canonical keyboard scrolling; do not add Timeline widget, progress, Carousel or selection semantics.
- Keep visible markers hidden from assistive technology because the native list already exposes position.
- Give informative images useful target-authored alternatives and decorative images empty alternatives.
- Keep the neutral supplementary-media fixture passive and out of the Tab order.
- A target-supplied player needs complete keyboard-accessible controls, captions, descriptions and transcripts as applicable.
- Do not autoplay or fetch third-party media by neutral-contract assumption.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** Process Timeline.

**Anatomy:** `.studio-tour.process-timeline` contextual root; required header and `.studio-tour__heading.process-timeline__title`; optional eyebrow and intro; required canonical `.process-timeline__track` and repeated `.process-step` composition; optional canonical step image and description; optional `.studio-tour__media`, media asset, and caption.

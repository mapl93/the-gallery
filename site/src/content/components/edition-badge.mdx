## Overview

Edition / Numbering is a passive, target-formatted statement with an optional designation, impression or piece identifier, edition size, and limited-edition emphasis.

## Presentation

The default variant has no modifier. `limited` maps to `.edition-badge--limited` and uses an editorial statement surface, not a warning state. Label, number, and total are optional because the target owns truthful records and complete localized formatting. The root is omitted when all three are blank.

The component is passive. It does not parse values or verify provenance, authenticity, scarcity, availability, produced quantity, or inventory. `42 / 100` is fixture content, not a universal record model.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Format the complete edition statement so it reads coherently in the target locale.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer authenticity, availability, or inventory guarantees from the limited treatment.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use `limited` only when the target can support that designation.
- Keep designation, impression identifier, and edition size truthful and synchronized with the target record.
- Keep proof vocabulary and localized wording complete instead of deriving it inside the component.
- Do not infer edition size from saleable inventory or a Shopify variant count.

## Accessibility

- Use the visible paragraph content as the accessible representation; do not add `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` to the paragraph.
- Keep the slash exposed when both number and total are present because it communicates their relationship.
- Do not rely on limited-edition color alone or expose warning, verification, live-region, or focus semantics.
- Use complete target wording and direction isolation for localized or mixed-direction values.

## Specification

Public visual tokens control designation/count spacing, number/separator spacing, block/inline padding, designation tracking and border thickness. The count gap initially aliases the outer gap and can be overridden independently. Spacing factors multiply the shared element gap; changing content or variants remains separate. Studio and Exhibit derive controls from the same public inventory.


**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** `.edition-badge` with optional `.edition-badge__label`, private `.edition-badge__count`, number, conditional separator, and total.

## Overview

Process Timeline presents target-owned stages in one intrinsic ordered lane. Equal tracks share available inline space and overflow horizontally only when the supplied steps no longer fit.

## Presentation

`title` and the ordered `steps` slot are required. Each direct list item exposes a visible sequence number and title, with optional image and description regions. Native ordered-list semantics provide the accessible position, so the duplicate visual number is hidden from assistive technology.

The one-row lane shares available inline space intrinsically and scrolls horizontally with snap alignment only when the supplied steps do not fit. Targets keep the lane keyboard-scrollable, associate it with the visible process heading, and leave native scroll keys untouched. Step records and their content remain target-owned.

Marker diameter, connector thickness, image ratio, step minimum width, focus,
spacing factors and the inline padding cap are public tokens. Connector vertical
placement is derived from marker diameter and thickness; it is not a second
position token. Its length follows the track gap. The initial 36px marker and
2px connector reproduce the previous 17px position. The 11rem step minimum is
bounded by the available lane width, preserving intrinsic native overflow.

Copy-and-own consumers explicitly adopt CSS/tokens. No source modes, semantic
properties, ordering model or runtime are added.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep steps in the real process order and use visible sequence numbers.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use connector lines or scroll position as the only indication of sequence.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep step titles parallel and descriptions concise.
- Include images only when they clarify a stage of the process.
- Preserve the same logical order across narrow and wide layouts.

## Accessibility

- Use a heading level appropriate to the surrounding page for the timeline title and each step title.
- Use native `ol`/`li` and preserve logical step order in the source regardless of available inline space.
- Give meaningful step images useful alternative text and mark decorative images accordingly.
- Make an overflowing track keyboard-scrollable with visible focus and a visible-heading label.
- Do not add progress, current-step, selection, Carousel, live-region, or custom key-handler semantics.

## Presentation

Without media, the statement surface pairs with primary text. With media, the overlay establishes contrast and the content switches to inverse text. `span-2` occupies two collection columns on wider viewports and returns to one on mobile.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Make the promoted destination distinct from neighboring products.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use the tile to disguise an advertisement as a Product Card.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep one clear destination and concise copy.
- Use meaningful media only when it adds to the promotion.
- Confirm that a two-column span still preserves the intended collection rhythm.

## Accessibility

- Keep CTA text descriptive outside its visual context.
- Provide alternative text only when media conveys information not present in copy.
- Verify overlay contrast across the complete crop range.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus on the CTA.

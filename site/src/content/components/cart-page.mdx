## Overview

Cart Page composes the ordered line items and the order summary into the full cart experience. It also provides the page title, optional item count, and optional continue-shopping link.

## Presentation

The root presents one column at narrow widths and places the summary beside the line-item region at the desktop breakpoint. `lineItems` and `summary` are required composition slots; Cart Page does not replace the behavior owned by Cart Line Item or Cart Summary.

The header contains the required title and may include a target-formatted item count. The continue-shopping link is optional and requires both its label and destination when present.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Update the item count, line items, and summary from the same cart state.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not let totals or quantities become stale after a cart mutation.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the line-item order consistent with the commerce target.
- Preserve a direct path back to shopping when the continue link is included.
- Let the target own loading, persistence, and checkout handoff behavior.

## Accessibility

- Use a page-level heading for `title`.
- Announce item-count and total changes when quantity or removal actions update the cart.
- Preserve logical reading order between the line-item region and the summary.
- Keep the continue-shopping link keyboard reachable with a visible focus indicator.

## Overview

Announcement Bar presents one concise, target-owned message above the global header. Its content may include a native link.

<Preview html={'<section class="announcement tg-preview-static" aria-label="Store announcement"><a href="#announcement-shipping">Complimentary shipping on orders over $150</a></section>'} label="Announcement Bar" />

## Presentation

The root owns the statement surface, centered text treatment, containment, and optional link styling. `message` is required slot content; `label` is optional when the bar is exposed as a labelled region.

Dismissal, rotation, countdowns, scheduling, targeting, persistence, and campaign analytics are not part of the accepted component API.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use one brief message with a destination that directly supports the claim.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not place critical checkout, consent, or legal information only in a promotional announcement.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the message readable without requiring animation.
- Use a native link only when the message navigates to a relevant destination.
- Keep campaign and scheduling policy in the consuming target.

## Accessibility

- Use `label` only when it adds useful context beyond the visible message.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus on linked announcements.
- Do not make essential information depend on automatic rotation or time-limited visibility.
- Keep the message understandable without color or decorative symbols.

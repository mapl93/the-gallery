## Overview

First-party passive editorial article that may occupy one Collection Grid item
without becoming a Product Card. It presents one required heading, optional
media and eyebrow, and at most one explicit canonical Link destination.
Sponsored or paid placement is outside the neutral v1 contract.

## Presentation

Without media, the statement surface uses primary text. With media, E6 adds one
passive bounded overlay and switches visible content to inverse text. The media
remains a native target-supplied image: use a concise contextual alternative
when it adds meaning and `alt=""` when it is decorative or repeats nearby copy.

`span-2` is a request to the owning Collection Grid, not a width on the article.
The parent `li.collection-grid__item` spans two tracks only when the Grid
container has at least two. In narrower containers the same source-order item
remains one track. Internal density follows the tile's own container rather than
the viewport.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Make the editorial destination and its relationship to the collection clear.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not disguise a paid placement or unrelated advertisement as a Product Card.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep one coherent editorial purpose, one heading and at most one destination.
- Pair `ctaLabel` and `href`; if either is absent, omit the complete CTA.
- Do not make the root clickable with JavaScript, pseudo-element overlays or
  duplicate links. The visible canonical Link is the only focus stop.
- Keep source order aligned with Collection Grid order. Do not use dense packing,
  CSS order or automatic position changes to feature the promotion.
- Omit the complete tile when the required title is empty. Do not invent copy,
  destination, image alternative or commercial disclosure.
- A target may omit E6 when it cannot preserve pagination, product counts,
  logical order, reflow or truthful promotional policy.
- The target owns eligible pages, insertion index, frequency, audience and
  source order. Inserting E6 must not alter product query, order, pagination,
  counts or filter/sort truth.

## Accessibility

- Render one passive `article` with one required visible heading. The owning
  page selects a heading rank appropriate to its document outline.
- Use a native `a[href]` with descriptive visible text. Pointer click and Enter
  retain browser navigation behavior and the root has no role or `tabindex`.
- Keep the overlay and directional icon `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Decide image alt from context: informative media gets concise meaning;
  decorative or redundant media gets empty alt.
- Verify all title, eyebrow and CTA text against the composited media crop, not
  only against named tokens. The no-media statement surface must also pass.
- Let long/localized and unbroken title/link text wrap. Preserve RTL source order,
  logical icon direction, visible focus, forced colors and 200% text/zoom reflow.
- E6 adds no animation or runtime; canonical Link removes its decorative
  transition under reduced motion.

WAI recommends native links with visible names and contextual image alternatives.
Open UI documents full-card click delegation as incubating and records tradeoffs
in current wrapper, overlay and JavaScript workarounds. The Gallery therefore
keeps the explicit native CTA.

## Overview

Artist Statement pairs optional portrait media with a required artist name and rich statement body, plus optional editorial label, quotation, and signature.

## Presentation

`name` and `body` are required. Portrait, editorial label, highlighted quotation, and signature are optional. Portrait, body, and signature remain target-owned rich content or media.

The default wide layout uses a sticky portrait beside the statement. It stacks on narrow viewports while preserving portrait and statement reading order; sticky positioning is visual only.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Preserve the artist's voice and keep the highlighted quotation within the surrounding statement context.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use the signature region as the only textual attribution.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use rich body content for the complete statement rather than placing all copy in the quotation.
- Keep portrait and signature media target-owned and editorially appropriate.
- Choose a heading level for the artist name that fits the surrounding page.

## Accessibility

- Use a heading level appropriate to the surrounding page for the artist name.
- Use quotation markup for the highlighted quote when it is a quotation.
- Give meaningful portrait media useful alternative text and mark decorative media accordingly.
- Treat a decorative signature image as decorative; provide text attribution when the signature conveys identity not otherwise present.

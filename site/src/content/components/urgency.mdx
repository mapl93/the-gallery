## Overview

Urgency presents one passive, text-complete commercial signal using an explicitly selected Low Stock, Selling Fast, Viewers, or Recent Sale claim type. The target owns the evidence, qualification, freshness, and publication decision.

## Presentation

<Preview html={'<p class="urgency urgency--low-stock"><span class="urgency__cue" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="urgency__message">Example only: 3 pieces remain</span></p>'} label="Urgency Indicator" />

The variant is required because no product claim default has been accepted. `.urgency--low-stock`, `.urgency--selling-fast`, `.urgency--viewers`, and `.urgency--recent-sale` are semantic claim classes, not interaction states or feedback severities. `.urgency__cue` is optional decoration; `.urgency__message` is required and carries the complete visible meaning.

G8 has no animation, live-region role, runtime fallback, controlled state, event, or neutral JavaScript. Trim blank messages and omit the complete root when the message or explicit claim type is invalid.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Select a claim explicitly from current target evidence and write a truthful, localized, self-contained message.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not invent thresholds, sales velocity, viewer counts, recency, a runtime default, or claims unsupported by the target.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The preview sentence demonstrates anatomy only. It is not a reusable claim, threshold, or default value.

## Accessibility

- Communicate the complete claim in visible text instead of relying on color or the optional decorative cue.
- Keep the passive indicator out of the keyboard focus order.
- Do not add `alert`, `status`, or another live role to content that is static or already present when the page loads.
- Verify source, scope, freshness, qualification, and omission behavior before exposing or updating a claim.
- G8 authors no motion; reduced-motion mode produces no component change.

## Overview

Cart Empty explains that no line items are present and may provide a primary recovery path back to shopping. Its icon, supporting message, and action are optional.

## Presentation

The required `title` is the empty-state identity. `icon` is an optional passive slot, `message` adds concise guidance, and the optional action is formed by `actionLabel` and `href` through the Button dependency.

Cart Empty composes the shared Empty State concept but keeps cart-specific recovery copy and destination in target-owned properties.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Show the empty state immediately after the final line item is removed.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not leave stale totals or checkout actions visible beside an empty cart.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Give the user a direct path back to shopping when a useful destination exists.
- Keep the message concise and specific to the empty cart.
- Treat the icon as supporting presentation, not the only explanation.

## Accessibility

- Use an appropriate heading level for `title` in the surrounding page.
- Keep the recovery action keyboard reachable with a descriptive label.
- Hide a decorative `icon` from assistive technology; provide an accessible alternative only when it adds meaning not present in text.
- Move focus deliberately after the final removal only when required by the surrounding cart flow.
- Announce the transition to an empty cart without duplicating the visible title.

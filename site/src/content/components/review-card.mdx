## Presentation

Required anatomy is the article root, flexible header, author metadata, composed Star Rating, and review body. Date and title are optional. Avatar, readable verification status, ordered photos, helpfulness action and count, and associated reply are optional target-owned regions.

The optional helpfulness action may expose local `aria-pressed` and native disabled state. Authentication, persistence, optimistic updates, deduplication, and count synchronization are not component behavior. Photos remain passive unless the target supplies a native link or button.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Render each review as a self-contained article in a logical author, rating, title, and body order.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer verification, generate dates, open a lightbox, or confirm helpfulness persistence from component presentation.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Review records, moderation, author identity, verification truth, replies, photos, and counts remain target data. A target that activates photos must separately choose the navigation, Lightbox, or Modal behavior.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Optional actions use native elements; supplied content remains readable without relying on icons, color, or hover.
</Callout>

- Use a semantic article for one review record.
- Render dates with `<time>` when a machine-readable value is available.
- Keep verification status readable as text and grounded in target data.
- Give informative photos meaningful alt text and decorative duplicates empty alt text.
- Use native links or buttons for photo actions and preserve visible focus.
- Put `aria-pressed` on the native helpfulness button, and do not announce unsynchronized counts as confirmed updates.

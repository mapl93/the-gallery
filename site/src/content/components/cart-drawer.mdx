## Presentation

The primary artwork uses Drawer for the visible overlay, panel, title, close control, body, and footer. The documentation fixture starts open; its close control and sibling trigger exercise only Drawer visibility, not a Cart Drawer commerce API.

`lineItems` is one optional ordered slot composed from the documented cart-item anatomy, Price, and Quantity Selector. `summary` is one optional target-formatted region. Both may be absent for an empty cart, whose composition remains unresolved.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep line records, formatted prices, quantity constraints, and totals synchronized with the target cart.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not calculate commerce truth or optimistic mutation policy inside Cart Drawer presentation.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Preserve product context in remove-action labels.
- Keep line-item destinations and selected variants truthful.
- Use the optional summary only when the target can update it with each cart mutation.

## Accessibility

- Inherit Drawer dialog naming, focus containment, dismissal, scroll locking, and focus restoration.
- Give each remove action an accessible name that includes its product context.
- Provide accessible names and native bounds for composed Quantity Selectors.
- Announce mutation, error, item-count, and total changes according to the target's cart policy.
- Keep product links and controls keyboard reachable with visible focus indicators.

## Overview

Product Info is a passive composition for product identity, canonical pricing,
semantic rich description, and name-value metadata.

## Presentation

The identity wrapper keeps vendor, title, and subtitle as one editorial cluster.
Price is a required canonical dependency. Description and metadata are optional
semantic slots; the renderer omits their wrappers when no content exists.

The component has one intrinsic size and no state or neutral runtime. Metadata
columns adapt to the component's available inline size without changing source or
reading order. Heading rank remains contextual: use `h1` on a full product page
when it is the page heading and the appropriate lower rank in a quick view or
other composition.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Supply one non-empty native title heading, one canonical Price, semantic rich description content, and complete localized metadata pairs.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not place Breadcrumb, Rating, Product Form, coordinator state, or update-announcement behavior inside Product Info merely because they appear nearby.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Under accepted direction D3-A, Product Info displays whichever truthful
projection the owning product surface supplies. Before resolution that may be a
target-formatted product Price range with product-level metadata only. After
resolution, Price, SKU, inventory and availability represent the exact selected
variant. The same page or Quick View coordinator atomically updates media, URL,
structured data and at most one localized status; Product Info never announces
independently.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Product Info is ordinary document content, not an ARIA widget. Native structure
  and visible text provide its semantics.
</Callout>

- Choose the title's native heading level from the surrounding document outline.
- Do not add a redundant `aria-label`, widget role, or tab stop to the root.
- Preserve paragraphs, headings, lists, links, emphasis, language, and direction
  in supplied rich description content.
- Use `dl` with one or more complete `dt`/`dd` groups for metadata; never render
  an empty description list.
- Use a known direction or `dir="auto"` for unknown plaintext identity and scalar
  metadata values. Price isolates its formatted values with `bdi`.
- Links inside rich content retain their native visible focus and keyboard
  behavior. Product Info itself adds no interaction or motion.
- Do not rely on color alone for price, availability, inventory, or status.

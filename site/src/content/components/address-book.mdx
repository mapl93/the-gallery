## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Pre-fill fields when possible (e.g., address from previous orders) to reduce friction.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't expose sensitive account data without authentication.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="warning">
  Account components require Shopify customer authentication. They render only for logged-in customers.
</Callout>

- Ensure proper color contrast ratios (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text) when customizing tokens.

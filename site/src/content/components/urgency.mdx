## Overview

Urgency presents a passive, text-complete claim using an explicitly selected Low Stock, Selling Fast, Viewers, or Recent Sale treatment.

## Presentation

<Preview html={'<p class="urgency urgency--low-stock">Verified inventory: 3 pieces remain</p>'} label="Urgency Indicator" />

The variant is required because no product default has been accepted. `.urgency--low-stock`, `.urgency--selling-fast`, `.urgency--viewers`, and `.urgency--recent-sale` are semantic presentation classes, not interaction states. Low Stock may generate a visual pulse; Selling Fast may include a supplied SVG; Viewers may emphasize text with `strong`.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Select a treatment explicitly from current, verified target data and write a truthful self-contained message.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not invent stock thresholds, viewer counts, sale recency, a runtime default, or claims unsupported by the target.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The preview sentence demonstrates anatomy only. It is not a reusable claim, threshold, or default value.

## Accessibility

- Communicate the complete claim in text instead of relying on color, icon, or motion.
- Keep the passive indicator out of the keyboard focus order.
- Verify stock, sales, and viewer claims before exposing them.
- Disable pulse animation when the user requests reduced motion.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use scroll area when a bounded region must preserve native scrolling.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't hide scrollbars when overflow content needs to be discoverable.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Scrollbar styling is progressive enhancement. Native scroll behavior should remain intact.
</Callout>

- Make the region focusable and named when keyboard users need to scroll it directly.
- Keep content inside the scroll area reachable by keyboard.
- Do not replace native scrolling with custom drag-only mechanics.

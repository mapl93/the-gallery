## Overview

On/off toggle switch. sm and lg sizes.

## Presentation

Switch supports `default`, `error`, `success`, and `warning` validation families independently of size and checked state.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Always pair form inputs with visible labels for accessibility.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't rely on placeholder text as the only label — it disappears on input.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Switch is backed by a native checkbox input. Targets may expose `role="switch"`, but checked state must stay synchronized with the native input.
</Callout>

- Provide a visible label or equivalent accessible name.
- Support Space to toggle the switch.
- Keep checked, disabled, and required state synchronized with the input.

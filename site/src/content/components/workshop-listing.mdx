## Presentation

`workshops` is the only property and owns the repeated card records. A card requires body content and a title; all media, schedule, level, description, footer, price, availability, and Button composition are optional.

Low-availability and full selectors are visual hooks paired with explicit text. Targets own schedules, currency formatting, price freshness, availability, destinations, ordering, and all booking behavior.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Show schedule, level, price, and availability as explicit target-formatted text.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not imply that a details or registration action reserves capacity or completes booking.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep workshop cards focused on summary information.
- Use a composed Button only for a target-owned destination or action.
- Confirm booking, payment, cancellation, and authentication behavior outside this contract.

## Accessibility

- Use semantic `time` markup with a machine-readable value when the target has one.
- Keep level, price, and availability available as text.
- Do not rely on warning or error color alone for availability.
- Preserve the composed Button's focus, disabled, and busy semantics.
- Give informative workshop media useful alternative text.

## Presentation

The required average group contains visible score and review-count text. A composed Star Rating is optional. Distribution is also optional and consists of repeated rows with a visible bucket label, passive track and target-sized fill, and visible count.

`ratingValue`, `scoreLabel`, `ratingLabel`, `reviewCount`, and `reviewCountLabel` are supplied independently by the target. The component does not calculate averages, normalize scales, select buckets, infer fill widths, or synchronize counts.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Supply readable score and count labels alongside any decorative stars or bars.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not present example distribution values as calculated component output.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Aggregation, rounding, missing values, filtering, moderation, localization, and provider synchronization remain open target decisions. Keep the visible labels consistent with the target data source.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Stars and bar widths supplement the supplied text; they are not the only representation of the data.
</Callout>

- Provide an accessible rating label containing the value and scale.
- Keep the numeric score and total review count visible as text.
- Give every distribution row a visible bucket label and count.
- Hide passive tracks and fills from assistive technology when their values are already expressed in text.
- Do not make passive aggregate data a live region unless the consuming target has a separate update requirement.

## References

**Anatomy:** required `.review-summary`, `.review-summary__average`, `.review-summary__score`, and `.review-summary__count`; optional composed `.star-rating`, `.review-summary__bars`, and repeated row, label, track, fill, and count elements.

**Dependencies:** None. Star Rating is an optional composition in the reviewed anatomy, but the current registry dependency array remains empty.

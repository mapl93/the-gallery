## Overview

Gift Wrap Option is one optional native order-level cart choice. It composes the canonical Checkbox and an optional canonical target-formatted Price; targets map confirmed selection to one explicit sellable gift-wrap service line at quantity one.

## Presentation

`label` is required and supplies visible accessible-name content. `selected` establishes initial native checkedness in static Web; a stateful commerce adapter controls the live property from its authoritative cart snapshot through the native `change` event. The optional `price` slot contains one complete Price and is omitted without implying that the service is free. A configured zero price remains explicit.

Gift Wrap owns only the cart-specific arrangement and target-mutation boundary. Checkbox owns selection, disabled state, keyboard, form value, focus, forced colors, and reduced motion. Price owns monetary parts, localization, bidirectional isolation, and formatting.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Reconcile checkedness and totals from one confirmed sellable gift-wrap line at quantity one.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not hide the charge in an attribute, create per-item wrapping implicitly, or treat optimistic checkedness as confirmed.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the option independent unless the product model explicitly introduces additional choices.
- Configure one dedicated target product or variant whose real Price, tax, inventory, shipping, fulfillment, discounts, refunds, and localization remain authoritative.
- Omit the complete component when the service is unavailable; use `disabled` only when a nearby explanation is useful.
- Use `name` and `value` only in targets whose native form mapping represents the accepted sellable-line request; Shopify's Ajax adapter uses variant identity instead.
- Keep pending, error, retry, status, focus, stale-response, and analytics behavior in the target cart lifecycle.

## Accessibility

- Keep one native checkbox and all non-interactive visible label content inside the wrapping label.
- Preserve Checkbox Space, checked, disabled, focus-visible, form-submit, and reset behavior.
- Keep the visible option label when Price is present; a price alone is not an accessible name.
- Reference external explanatory or unavailable content through `describedBy` when needed.
- Announce confirmed total or cart changes from the owning target region, not from K11 itself, and reconcile every rendered surface from the same cart snapshot.

## Overview

Related Articles presents an optional heading and a target-owned collection of Article Card compositions. Recommendation selection remains target-owned.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Let the target select relevant articles and pass complete Article Card compositions.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not add next or previous article behavior to this component while `.article-nav` ownership is undecided.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The adjacent `.article-nav` CSS family is not part of the Related Articles semantic property surface. Whether it belongs here or becomes a separate registered component remains unresolved.

## Accessibility

- Use a section heading level that fits the surrounding article page hierarchy.
- Keep the section label associated through its visible heading.
- Preserve every Article Card heading, destination, image alternative text, and focus behavior.
- Use `alt=""` when a linked card image only duplicates its adjacent linked title.
- Do not imply next or previous navigation semantics through visual placement alone.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use the article body/prose component for rich text — it handles typography, images, and spacing.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't manually style article content with inline styles — let the prose class handle it.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Blog components support Shopify's article template system. Use metafields for custom article data.
</Callout>

- Use `<button>` elements for actions and `<a>` for navigation. Never use `<div>` with a click handler.

## Overview

Article Card presents one self-contained native article summary on the
canonical Card surface. One native Link contains its contextual media, passive
summary content, and complete visible title as one destination. Passive category
metadata, author destinations, and future conflicting controls stay outside
that Link.

`variant` is the independent layout axis. `standard` has no Article Card
modifier; `featured`, `minimal`, `horizontal`, and `editorial` map to
`.article-card--featured`, `.article-card--minimal`,
`.article-card--horizontal`, and `.article-card--editorial`. They respond to the
card's own inline-size container.

`surface` composes canonical Card: `default` uses `.card`, `flat` adds
`.card--flat`, and `elevated` adds `.card--elevated`. Article Card does not copy
Card border, shadow, radius, color, hover, focus-within, or reduced-motion
properties.

`excerptLines` is `none | 2 | 3 | 4`. `none` renders the complete excerpt;
numeric values apply an explicit visual CSS line clamp while preserving the
complete supplied source text in DOM.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use one complete Link for media and title, compose Card and Badge canonically, and keep independent destinations outside that Link.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not forward root clicks, nest controls inside the primary Link, duplicate Card visual properties, or mutate excerpt source content to fit the card.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use `minimal` for text-led lists, `horizontal` for compact rows, `featured`
  for higher-emphasis placements, and `editorial` for the accent title profile.
- Choose Card surface independently from the layout profile.
- Keep category as a passive canonical Badge outside the primary Link.
- Keep target-formatted metadata passive inside the Link. Put author Links,
  taxonomy Links, and future controls outside it.
- Omit optional parts instead of rendering empty wrappers.
- Compose canonical Skeleton externally while loading and replace it with
  Article Card only when authoritative data is ready. L1 has no loading variant,
  `aria-busy` lifecycle, or internal Skeleton markup.

## Accessibility

- Use `article` and a contextual heading level that fits the surrounding page.
- Render exactly one native primary Link containing the media and complete
  visible title. It must have no nested interactive descendants.
- Use useful image alternative text when media adds information and `alt=""`
  only when decorative or redundant in context.
- Use machine-readable `time[datetime]` values and hide visual-only separators.
- Treat excerpt fitting as visual only. Do not place essential differentiating
  information exclusively after a clamp point.
- Preserve full-Link focus, native Tab/Enter/context-menu/modifier behavior,
  forced colors, coarse pointers, and canonical Card reduced motion.

## Presentation

`title` is required. Hero media, label, dates, location, narrative description, metadata details, featured works, and participating artists are optional target-owned content. The composition preserves hero, information, works, and artist reading order as its responsive grids stack.

The featured-work and participating-artist slots intentionally use passive fixture records in the artwork. ADR 0080 does not decide whether Product Card, Artist Card, links, or another dependency owns those records or interactions.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep the page-level narrative order stable and let each target supply exhibition records.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not assign Product Card, Artist Card, or activation ownership to the open composition slots.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use the hero for the exhibition identity, date, and location rather than unrelated promotion.
- Present exhibition metadata as clear name-value pairs.
- Keep works and participating artists as target-owned compositions until their ownership boundary is accepted.

## Accessibility

- Use one page-level heading for the exhibition title and logical headings for optional works and artist regions.
- Give meaningful hero and artist media useful alternative text and mark decorative media accordingly.
- Use semantic name-value markup for exhibition details and machine-readable date markup where the target can provide it.
- Composed works and artist items retain their own link, control, focus, and accessible-name requirements.

## References

**Dependencies:** None. Product Card and Artist Card are not dependencies while record and interaction ownership remains unresolved.

**Anatomy:** required `.exhibition-page`, `.exhibition-hero`, `.exhibition-hero__content`, and `.exhibition-hero__title`; optional hero media, image, overlay, label, dates, and location; optional `.exhibition-info` description and metadata anatomy; optional `.exhibition-works` heading and grid; optional `.exhibition-artists` heading, list, item, avatar, name, and role.

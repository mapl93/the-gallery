## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Hover Card for a short passive preview whose complete information is available at the trigger link destination.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't place required instructions, controls, destructive actions, or unique information only inside a Hover Card.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Hover Card must be dismissible, hoverable, and persistent under WCAG 1.4.13. Use Popover when content needs explicit activation or interaction.
</Callout>

- Ensure the trigger can receive keyboard focus.
- Keep the surface visible while pointer or focus remains within trigger/content.
- Let `Escape` apply `.hover-card--dismissed` for the current interest cycle; reset after pointer and focus leave.
- Keep the content supplemental and available through another path on touch-first targets.
- Do not trap focus inside a hover card.

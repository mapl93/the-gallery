## Overview

Read-only projection of a target-supplied rating from zero through five in half-step increments. Star Rating does not collect a value or calculate an aggregate.

## Presentation

The root contains five decorative stars. Empty is the default star state; `.is-filled` or `data-filled="true"` marks a full star, and `.is-half` or `data-filled="half"` marks a half star. The default size is 16px and `.star-rating--lg` selects the 24px presentation.

`ratingValue` is supplied by the target and maps to `data-rating`. The accessible `label` states both value and scale. Neither property implies aggregation, rounding, or normalization of provider data.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Star Rating wherever a supplied rating is presented without value entry.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use Star Rating as an interactive picker or infer an aggregate from the decorative stars.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Use [Star Rating (Interactive)](/components/star-input) when a person must choose an integer rating. Whether non-five-point sources or increments other than half stars are supported remains a target product decision.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  The root exposes the complete rating as accessible text; each repeated star is decorative.
</Callout>

- Include the supplied value and scale in the root label, such as `4.5 out of 5 stars`.
- Hide individual stars from assistive technology with `aria-hidden="true"` when the root already names the rating.
- Keep a readable value wherever color or fill alone would be ambiguous.
- Do not add radio, slider, or button semantics to this passive component.

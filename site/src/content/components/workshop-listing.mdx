## Overview

Workshop Listing presents one required target-authored collection of independently complete workshop summaries. It is a discovery surface, not an event schema, capacity service, booking flow, checkout, calendar, filter, or live inventory region.

## Presentation

The required `workshops` slot supplies one or more target-owned records in reader order. A record needs stable identity and a visible non-empty title. Invalid or duplicate-identity peers omit; no valid records omit the complete list. The host may compose Empty State outside R7 when absence needs an explanation or recovery action.

R7 uses native `ul > li > article` structure. Every article is named by its visible contextual heading. Heading rank follows the surrounding document and is not a visual variant. The whole card is not one interactive wrapper around its nested destination or future actions.

Each article composes canonical Card. Media, schedule, level/format, description, footer, Price, availability Badge, and Button action are optional only when the remaining summary is truthful and useful.

### Schedule and commerce boundaries

Schedule text is complete target-localized content. Use native `time[datetime]` only when the target has a valid machine-readable occurrence that corresponds to the visible text. R7 does not choose an occurrence, time zone, locale, recurrence, cancellation or stale-data fallback.

Price consumes canonical Price and arrives as one complete target-formatted string. Availability consumes passive canonical Badge and remains explicit visible text. `lowAvailability` and `full` select supporting Warning/Error treatments; capacity thresholds, freshness and announcements stay target-owned.

### Destination and booking boundaries

A details destination composes canonical Button as native `a[href]`, includes the workshop title in its accessible name, and preserves ordinary link behavior. A Full workshop can still expose details, waitlist, cancellation or later sessions, so Full does not automatically disable the details link.

R7 owns no registration, reservation, authentication, payment, cancellation, error, confirmation or inventory-lock behavior. A target may compose a separately certified booking action only after those workflow decisions exist.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Supply complete schedule, price, availability and destination facts, and keep each article understandable from its visible title and text.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use a no-op button, disable details solely because a workshop is full, or imply that viewing a card reserves capacity.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Preserve target order and omit incomplete records rather than displaying placeholder cards.
- Use canonical Card, Price, Badge and Button; do not recreate their surface, currency, status, focus or disabled behavior.
- Keep availability thresholds and freshness in the authoritative target service.
- Include time zone, cancellation/reschedule, prerequisites, venue, access or materials when omitting them would mislead.
- Keep informative media alternatives contextual; use empty alt only when media is decorative or fully redundant.
- Compose filtering, pagination, loading, errors and empty results outside R7.

## Accessibility

- Preserve native `ul > li > article` collection semantics and a visible contextual heading for every article.
- Use `time[datetime]` only with corresponding valid machine-readable schedule content.
- Keep schedule, level, price and availability available as visible text and do not rely on tone, position or media alone.
- Use canonical Price `bdi` isolation for formatted currency and allow long localized content to wrap.
- Use native anchors for navigation; keep the visible action label inside the accessible name and add workshop-title context for repeated links.
- Do not make the entire card clickable around nested controls or add grid/widget roles, roving focus or arrow-key behavior.
- Keep static availability out of live regions. A target that updates capacity owns announcement timing and deduplication.
- Preserve canonical Card/Button focus and reduced-motion behavior across RTL, text enlargement and forced colors.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** canonical [Card](/components/card), [Price](/components/price), [Badge](/components/badge), and [Button](/components/button).

**Anatomy:** required native `.workshop-grid`; repeated direct `.workshop-grid__item`; native `.card.workshop-card` article; optional `.card__media.workshop-card__image`; required `.card__body.workshop-card__body` and contextual `.workshop-card__title`; optional `.workshop-card__date`, level and description; optional `.card__footer.workshop-card__footer` containing canonical Price, Badge and/or Button-as-anchor composition.

**Targets**

- **Neutral Web:** native list/articles/time/link, canonical Card/Price/Badge/Button, intrinsic CSS and zero R7 JavaScript.
- **Shopify:** planned until workshop resource/metaobject, schedule/time-zone/locale, money/capacity freshness, destination/booking service, editor validation and first consumer are approved.
- **Webflow:** CMS supplies ordered complete records, valid schedule metadata, formatted price/status and real destinations.
- **React / Angular:** thin target projection; canonical descendants retain their semantics and booking state remains external.
- **Figma:** future component models one/many records, media absent/present, sparse/dense metadata, Available/Limited/Full and narrow/wide modes; current generic references are not R7 approval.

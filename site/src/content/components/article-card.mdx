## Overview

Article Card presents an article destination with a required title and optional media, category, metadata, excerpt, and author content. Media, metadata, and author identity remain target-owned slots.

The default `standard` presentation has no modifier class. `featured`, `minimal`, `horizontal`, and `editorial` map to `.article-card--featured`, `.article-card--minimal`, `.article-card--horizontal`, and `.article-card--editorial`. The parent layout controls the card width.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep the media and title links pointed at the same article destination.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not turn target-formatted dates, reading time, taxonomy, or author data into root-level component behavior.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Use `minimal` for text-led lists, `horizontal` for compact rows, `featured` for higher-emphasis placements, and `editorial` when the accent title treatment is appropriate. The skeleton selector is presentation evidence only: whether it belongs to Article Card or the standalone Skeleton component remains unresolved.

## Accessibility

- Use an `article` element and a heading level that fits the surrounding page hierarchy.
- Keep media and title as separate links instead of wrapping the complete card in one link.
- Use useful image alternative text when media adds information. Use `alt=""` when linked media only repeats the adjacent title, as in the preview.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus and do not rely on hover lift or image zoom to communicate the destination.

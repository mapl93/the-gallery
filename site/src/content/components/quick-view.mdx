## Overview

Quick View is a focused product-decision profile of canonical Modal. It opens only from one complete target-coordinated product snapshot and composes the full canonical rich-media Product Gallery, target-formatted Price, an optional native Product Form, and an optional Link to the complete product page without becoming a second product page or commerce controller.

## Presentation

The product title is both the visible Modal title and its accessible name. The body keeps a stable source order—Gallery, then product information and continuations—and switches its internal columns from the Quick View container rather than the page viewport.

`gallery`, `price`, `title`, and `dismissLabel` are required. `vendor` and a concise target-authored `description` are optional. A valid Quick View provides `productForm`, a coherent `fullLinkLabel`/`href` pair, or both. One-sided Link values, a blank title, or no continuation are invalid and should omit the incomplete surface instead of inventing markup or URLs.

Modal owns overlay anatomy and the visible Close Button. Product Gallery owns images, hosted video, external video, models, selection, direct swipe, explicit activation/release and alternatives. Inside Quick View the same Gallery is configured with `imageDetail="none"`: images are passive and never open or transfer a nested Lightbox. Price owns target-formatted monetary presentation. Product Form owns its native form and child action states. Link owns native navigation and focus. K8 owns only the domain composition and its private responsive layout.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use Quick View for a focused decision that needs less information than the complete product page.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not duplicate every product-page section, silently choose a variant, or report local purchase success before the target confirms it.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Open Quick View only after one target product coordinator has a complete coherent product snapshot. Omit it while data is incomplete, loading, stale or cannot be synchronized.
- Keep selected options, purchasable merchandise id, the full rich-media Gallery and current media id, Price, availability, quantity rules, URL, Product Form and result state synchronized through that coordinator.
- Keep image selection, direct swipe, explicit video activation and model interaction available. Do not add nested image detail; the complete-product destination uses Product Gallery's default `imageDetail="lightbox"` experience.
- Use the complete-product Link for specifications, policies, customization and supplementary fields that remain outside the focused modal.
- Keep native Product Form submission available as a progressive baseline; Ajax purchase, Cart Drawer refresh, retry, analytics and result status remain target behavior.
- Treat mobile full-screen/sheet presentation, accelerated checkout, selling plans, line-item properties and one-of-one quantity policy as product decisions rather than generic K8 controls.
- Do not expose modal width, breakpoint, gap, media ratio, close placement, focus selector, portal or description-line count as public properties.

## Accessibility

- Present K8 through the complete Modal lifecycle: outside content inert, content-aware initial focus, contained Tab/Shift+Tab, Escape dismissal, a visible named close action, and logical focus restoration.
- Assert `aria-modal="true"` only when modality is real. Native `<dialog>.showModal()` is the preferred Web mapping when the target architecture permits it.
- Reference the required visible product title with `aria-labelledby`. Do not add `aria-describedby` for a structurally rich product composition that should be read element by element.
- Preserve Product Gallery alternatives, named selection controls, current-media status, explicit rich-media activation/release and native fallbacks; canonical Price bidirectional isolation; native Product Form validity/submission; and native Link activation.
- Keep exactly one modal dialog. Featured images are passive in K8 and cannot open a nested or transferred Lightbox.
- Use at most one deliberate target-owned localized status for coordinated media/price/availability/purchase updates; K8 is not itself a live region.
- Honor reduced motion and forced colors, preserve logical direction, and contain long localized or unbroken content at effective 200 percent zoom.

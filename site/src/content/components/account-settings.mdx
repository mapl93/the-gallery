## Presentation

`sections` is the only Account Settings property. Section titles, descriptions, controls, actions, ordering, validation, and persistence are repeated fixture or target-owned composition inside that slot.

The artwork demonstrates Input, Switch, and Button dependencies without establishing whether a target saves on submit, on change, or by another section-specific strategy. Account Settings supplies grouping and visual structure only.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Group related preferences under concise titles and explain consequences where needed.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not make the wrapper responsible for child-control validation or persistence policy.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Let each target decide which sections and controls are available.
- Use Input and Switch validation semantics for field-level feedback.
- Make destructive account actions visually and verbally explicit and confirm them according to target policy.
- Keep persistence, loading, success, and failure behavior consistent within the chosen target strategy.

## Accessibility

- Protect account settings behind the target authentication system.
- Give every section a descriptive heading and preserve logical heading order.
- Associate controls with visible labels and supporting descriptions where needed.
- Keep Switch checked state synchronized with its native checkbox.
- Announce save failures or confirmations through target-owned feedback and move focus appropriately after errors.

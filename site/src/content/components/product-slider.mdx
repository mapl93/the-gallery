## Overview

Product Slider is a finite, user-controlled horizontal product list for related,
recommended, complementary, or recently viewed products. It composes the
canonical Carousel scroll model, Icon Button controls, and Product Card items;
the owning target supplies the product collection and any recommendation data.

## Presentation

The visible heading names both the contextual section and its focusable native
list. The track shows two cards initially, three at a 48rem component container,
and four at 64rem. Those thresholds respond to the component container, not the
page viewport. Header text wraps without colliding with controls, and Product
Card retains ownership of media, typography, Price, badges, focus, and actions.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Supply a short, meaningful, non-empty product collection and let every item render the canonical Product Card.</Do>
  <Dont>Use Product Slider as an autoplaying hero, infinite feed, or replacement for a collection grid.</Dont>
  <Do>Keep previous and next labels localized and synchronized with real finite bounds.</Do>
  <Dont>Duplicate Product Card markup or page by a physical fraction of the track width.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Recommendation source, loading, empty/error handling, recently viewed storage,
inventory, quick-add results, analytics, and pagination belong to the target or
application. Omit the component when no valid item exists.

## Accessibility

- Render a contextual heading and reference it from the section and track.
- Preserve native `ul`/`li` semantics and normal Product Card link/Button order.
- Make the native overflow track keyboard focusable; do not intercept arrow keys
  at the component root.
- Use canonical Icon Buttons with localized names, `aria-controls`, and native
  `disabled` bounds. Activation keeps focus on the control while another item is
  reachable; if the control becomes disabled at a bound, focus moves to the named
  track instead of falling back to the document.
- Synchronize first/middle/last bounds after explicit, touch, wheel, scrollbar,
  or keyboard scrolling.
- Target exact adjacent items with logical scrolling so gaps, responsive card
  counts, variable text, and RTL do not depend on physical offsets.
- Do not autoplay or loop in v1. Canonical Carousel supplies visible focus,
  reduced-motion, forced-colors, and RTL behavior.

## Technical Specification

Product Slider layers public dependency classes rather than copying behavior:

- root: `.carousel.product-slider`
- track: `.carousel__track.product-slider__track`
- item: `.carousel__slide.product-slider__item`
- controls: `.icon-btn.icon-btn--round.carousel__nav-btn.product-slider__nav-button`
- item content: one `.card.product-card` per list item

The target owns current item and overflow as derived state. Framework adapters
may provide controlled and uncontrolled current IDs when application coordination
requires them, but both modes must serialize to one scroll position and one pair
of finite disabled bounds.

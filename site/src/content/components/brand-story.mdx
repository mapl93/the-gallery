## Overview

Brand Story is a passive brand-narrative profile composed from canonical Image with Text. It requires target-owned media, a visible contextual heading, and a non-empty narrative body; an eyebrow and editorial signature are optional.

Brand Story consumes Image with Text's native section, media/content anatomy, source order, intrinsic response, crop, typography, color, and spacing. It adds only an optional closing attribution or editorial mark. The profile does not expose Image with Text's variants or action slot.

`media`, `title`, and `body` are required. Invalid required composition is omitted completely. `eyebrow` and `signature` are independently optional and leave no empty wrappers or rhythm when absent.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use one specific origin, philosophy, or practice narrative whose media adds useful context to the written story.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use Brand Story as a generic promotional banner, artist biography, authenticity record, or second Image with Text implementation.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the written narrative understandable without depending on the media.
- Use Artist Profile for biography, Artist Statement for a first-person artist-authored statement, and Collection Story for collection-scoped narrative.
- Keep the optional signature editorial. It must not imply verified authorship, authenticity, approval, or cryptographic proof.
- Do not expose image side, ratio, crop, alignment, padding, content width, signature medium, or heading rank as neutral S8 properties.

## Accessibility

- Render only with target-owned media, a non-empty visible title, and non-empty body content.
- Associate the native section with the visible contextual heading through one target-unique `aria-labelledby` and `id` pair.
- Give informative media useful target-authored alternative text and intrinsic dimensions; use `alt=""` for decorative or redundant media.
- Preserve media before narrative in DOM/source order at every width and in RTL.
- Preserve semantic paragraphs, lists, genuine quotations, links, and headings inside the target-owned body.
- Render a supplied signature as ordinary visible footer text or purpose-labelled target media. Do not replace visible signature text with a redundant ARIA label.
- Brand Story adds no focus stop, widget role, key handling, live region, authored motion, or neutral runtime.

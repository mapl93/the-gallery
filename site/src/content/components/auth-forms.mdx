## Overview

Auth Forms is a narrow authentication shell for a target-owned account entry flow. It provides the heading, form stack, password-recovery action, authentication-method divider, social actions, and footer composition without defining a login/register mode.

## Presentation

The artwork uses a sign-in fixture, but the contract defines a neutral shell rather than a canonical login/register mode. `form`, `forgotAction`, `socialActions`, and `footer` are composition slots whose fields, routes, providers, submission, and account-mode navigation remain target-owned.

`title` is required. The supporting `subtitle`, divider, recovery action, social actions, and footer may be omitted independently when they do not apply to the target flow.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Compose the canonical Input, Button, and Form behavior inside the form slot.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not duplicate field validation or authentication policy in the Auth Forms shell.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the visible title aligned with the target flow being presented.
- Include password recovery where the authentication method supports it.
- Treat social providers, routes, errors, loading, and submission as target integration concerns.
- Login and registration entry points may be public; access to resulting account data remains controlled by the target authentication system.

## Accessibility

- Associate every input with a visible label and connect validation messages with `aria-describedby`.
- Give each social authentication action a provider-specific accessible name.
- Keep password recovery and account-mode navigation keyboard reachable with visible focus.
- Announce authentication errors without revealing sensitive account existence information.
- Preserve native form submission semantics and appropriate autocomplete values.

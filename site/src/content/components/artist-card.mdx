## Overview

Artist Card is a passive artist summary with optional portrait, canonical Badge, medium, location, and target-formatted piece count around a required artist name.

## Presentation

`name` is required. Portrait, Badge, medium, location, and target-formatted piece count are optional and should be omitted when empty. Badge is rendered through the canonical Badge component and appears only with a portrait. The card follows the width of its containing layout.

The shared artwork is deliberately passive. ADR 0080 does not decide whether Artist Card is always navigational, optionally interactive, or passive in some compositions. The contract therefore exposes no `href` or activation property. Targets may use a native `a[href]` or `button` root for one coherent activation; only enabled native roots receive pointer and hover motion, while native roots preserve visible keyboard focus and disabled button semantics.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use the passive card until the surrounding target has made its interaction model explicit.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not add click handling to a passive container or assume every card navigates.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep Badge text short and use it only for meaningful artist context over a present portrait.
- Format the piece count in the target rather than deriving grammar in the card.
- When activation is decided, use native link or button markup and avoid nested controls.
- Keep the canonical Flat Card classes on the root; Artist Card owns its content anatomy and optional native interaction, while Card owns the shared shell.

## Accessibility

- Choose portrait alternative text from its purpose: describe informative portraits concisely and use empty alternative text when decorative or redundant.
- When the target makes the card interactive, use native link or button markup instead of click handling on a passive container.
- Ensure an interactive card has a descriptive accessible name, visible focus, and no nested interactive controls.
- Choose the artist-name heading level from the containing document outline, omit empty optional elements, and do not rely on portrait hover motion to communicate identity or availability.

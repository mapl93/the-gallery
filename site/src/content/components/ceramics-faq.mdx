## Presentation

`items` is required. `heading`, `subheading`, `categories`, and `contact` are optional. Every item requires a disclosure-style question and answer.

The artwork demonstrates static current-category and disclosure relationships only. Targets own questions, answers, ordering, destinations, category selection mode, filtering or navigation, URL state, result announcements, and disclosure behavior. The contract does not yet compose canonical Accordion.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep answers adjacent to their questions and make category state explicit when controls are used.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer filtering, navigation, URL synchronization, or Accordion ownership from existing visual hooks.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use categories only after the target defines whether they filter in place or navigate.
- Keep follow-up destinations descriptive and target-owned.
- Use answer content appropriate to the specific product or policy source.

## Accessibility

- Synchronize each question's `aria-expanded` value with its answer visibility.
- Reference each answer panel with `aria-controls` and label the panel from its question.
- Keep questions and answers adjacent in source order.
- Expose the current category and announce result changes when categories filter content.
- Do not rely on category color alone to communicate selection.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None. Accordion composition remains unresolved.

**Anatomy:** `.ceramics-faq`, optional header, heading, subheading, category region and controls, required list with repeated `.faq-item`, question, and answer anatomy, and optional contact text and target-owned link.

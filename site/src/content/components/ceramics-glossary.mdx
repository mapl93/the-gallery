## Presentation

`terms` is required. `title` and `intro` are optional. Every group requires a contextual letter heading and one or more canonical Accordion items. Every item requires a visible `dfn` term and definition; informative media and related Links are optional.

The artwork starts with one definition expanded so the content, media, and related-link composition stay reviewable. Targets own records, normalized grouping keys, source order, stable ids, initial state, single-or-multiple expansion policy, and real destinations.

Alphabet navigation is intentionally absent from the v1 API. Navigation, filtering, search, URL state, focus movement, and result announcements remain an open product decision rather than an inert visual control.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Compose Accordion and Link instead of duplicating their markup, focus, visibility, or motion behavior.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not add alphabet controls until their navigation or filtering contract is explicitly defined.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep groups and terms in meaningful source order.
- Use `dfn` for each visible term and plain flow content for its definition.
- Use real related-term destinations; do not render placeholder or prevented links.
- Consider an always-visible `dl` presentation when user evidence does not support disclosure interaction.

## Accessibility

- Fit title, group, and Accordion headings into the surrounding document hierarchy.
- Synchronize trigger ids, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, panel ids, optional region labels, and native `hidden` state.
- Preserve native Button click, Enter, Space, and normal Tab order.
- Avoid region landmark proliferation when a target can expose many panels at once.
- Give informative media useful alternative text and keep decorative media empty.
- Verify long and localized terms at narrow widths, 200 percent zoom, and right-to-left direction.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** Accordion, Link.

**Anatomy:** `.ceramics-glossary`, optional title and intro, required repeated groups and letter headings, `.ceramics-glossary__terms.accordion`, `.accordion__item.glossary-term`, canonical Accordion heading/trigger/panel/content, optional image, related sentence, and canonical Link.

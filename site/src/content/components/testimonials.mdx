## Overview

Testimonials presents a responsive collection of attributed quotations with optional Avatar composition.

The collection has an optional section title and a required repeated-card grid. Each card contains semantic quotation content and a visible author name; the author row may compose Avatar without replacing that text identity.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Publish attributable testimonials whose wording and source have been verified.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not fabricate endorsements, obscure material edits, or turn passive testimony into an unexplained control.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep each quotation focused and preserve enough author detail for the endorsement to be understandable in context.

## Accessibility

- Use semantic quotation markup and keep the generated opening quote decorative.
- Keep every author name visible when Avatar is composed.
- Treat testimonial cards as passive content unless a separately contracted control is added.
- Use list semantics when the cards form a meaningful collection.

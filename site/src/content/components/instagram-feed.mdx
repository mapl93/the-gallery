## Overview

Instagram Feed arranges target-owned social media items beneath optional account context. Authorization, fetching, caching, consent, freshness, failures, metrics, and item activation remain target-owned.

The feed is a presentation shell for supplied records. Overlay metadata may be present, but social metrics are not guaranteed semantic properties. Items may be passive or receive target-selected native semantics.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Provide a resilient local or cached fallback and make every supplied destination explicit.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not imply that provider access, consent, fetching, or item navigation is built into the component.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep account context accurate and avoid making essential content available only through an external feed.

## Accessibility

- Give informative media useful alternative text and keep essential captions available without hover.
- Use a native link for the account handle only when a real profile destination exists.
- Give target-supplied item links or controls accessible names and visible focus.
- Communicate loading, empty, stale, and error conditions through the target data layer.

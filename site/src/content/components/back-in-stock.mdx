## Overview

Email signup form for an out-of-stock product notification. It keeps the email field, validation feedback, submit action, and submitted confirmation within one semantic component.

## Presentation

The field follows the shared value-entry language. `default`, `error`, `success`, and `warning` alter the label, border, indicator, focus ring, and message as a single validation family. The submitted state replaces the form with a concise live confirmation.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use one clear promise about what notification the customer will receive.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not hide delivery expectations or reuse the address for unrelated marketing without consent.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the message short enough to remain attached visually to the field.
- Use the warning state for recoverable attention, error for invalid input, and success only after validation.
- Keep the submit label specific to the notification action.

## Accessibility

- Connect the visible label and helper message to the email input.
- Announce submitted and validation feedback through an appropriate live region.
- Preserve the field while correcting an error; only replace it after successful submission.
- Do not use the indicator icon as the only carrier of validation meaning.

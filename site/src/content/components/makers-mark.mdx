## Overview

Maker's Mark is a passive identity surface for a visible maker name with optional stamp media, studio text, and target-formatted year.

## Presentation

`artist` is required. `stamp`, `studio`, and `year` are optional target-owned content. The surface is always passive and does not define artist or studio record models.

The fixture identifies a maker but makes no authenticity or verification claim.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Keep the maker's name visible as text and use the stamp as supporting identity content.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not present the mark as proof of authenticity unless a target owns and substantiates that claim.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use target-formatted studio and year text when provided.
- Keep the identity concise enough for the compact surface.
- Omit decorative stamp media when it adds no useful context.

## Accessibility

- Keep the maker identity available as text.
- Give an informative stamp useful alternative text.
- Hide a decorative stamp from assistive technology.
- Do not encode identity or provenance only in the image.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** required `.makers-mark` and `.makers-mark__info`, optional stamp, required artist name, and optional studio and year text.

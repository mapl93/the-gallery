## Overview

Related Articles is one passive, fail-closed, named thematic section containing one to three explicitly curated canonical Article Card summaries. It requires a non-empty visible title and at least one complete Article Card; missing either omits the complete section. The target removes the current article and duplicates while preserving curator order.

The root is a native `.related-articles` section named by its visible contextual heading through `aria-labelledby`. `.related-articles__grid` is a native `ul`; each direct `.related-articles__item` is an `li` containing exactly one complete canonical Article Card.

The visual grid uses intrinsic `auto-fit` sizing, so columns follow the component's available inline space rather than the viewport. A narrow host may show one column while another L10 instance in the same viewport shows two or three. Column count and the private minimum card measure are not public semantic properties.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Supply a truthful localized heading and one to three explicitly curated complete canonical Article Cards in curator order.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not infer a tag fallback, automatic ranking or personalization in v1, and never include the current article or duplicates.</Dont>
</DoDont>

<DoDont>
  <Do>Omit the complete section when no valid recommendations are available.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not interpret legacy `.article-nav` as L10 or register Previous/Next Article Navigation in v1.</Dont>
</DoDont>

The target owns the explicit curated records, current-article and duplicate exclusion, freshness, loading/error/no-result lifecycle, destinations, tracking, analytics and SEO. On article pages, the host places L10 after primary content and before Comments; Author Card and Share Actions may sit around that boundary without changing L10. The neutral component owns only named collection semantics and intrinsic layout.

Fixture article titles, categories, images, alternatives, destinations, order, item count, H2/H3 ranks and “Continue reading” copy are evidence content, not defaults or public API.

## Accessibility

- Require a non-empty visible heading and at least one complete Article Card; omit invalid composition instead of emitting an unlabelled or empty region.
- Use `section[aria-labelledby]`, a target-unique heading id, `ul` and direct `li` children without redundant roles or custom collection keyboard behavior.
- Preserve each canonical Article Card's native article summary, contextual heading, single required primary Link containing its contextual media and complete title, media alternative, Badge and optional metadata.
- Keep native Link Tab, Enter, context-menu and modifier-key behavior. The root, list and list items are passive and unfocusable.
- Preserve complete localized text, logical RTL flow, narrow-container reflow, effective 200% type, user text spacing, dark/forced-color behavior and canonical focus visibility.

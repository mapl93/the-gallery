## Overview

Artist Card is a compact artist summary with optional portrait, badge, medium, location, and piece count around a required artist name.

## Presentation

`name` is required. Portrait, badge, medium, location, and target-formatted piece count are optional. The card follows the width of its containing layout and applies portrait emphasis only for fine-pointer hover or focus within target-owned interaction.

The artwork is deliberately passive. ADR 0080 does not decide whether Artist Card is always navigational, optionally interactive, or passive in some compositions. The contract therefore exposes no `href` or activation property.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use the passive card until the surrounding target has made its interaction model explicit.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not add click handling to a passive container or assume every card navigates.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep badge text short and use it only for meaningful artist context.
- Format the piece count in the target rather than deriving grammar in the card.
- When activation is decided, use native link or button markup and avoid nested controls.

## Accessibility

- Give meaningful portraits useful alternative text and mark decorative portraits accordingly.
- When the target makes the card interactive, use native link or button markup instead of click handling on a passive container.
- Ensure an interactive card has a descriptive accessible name, visible focus, and no nested interactive controls.
- Do not rely on portrait hover motion to communicate identity or availability.

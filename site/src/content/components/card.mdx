## Presentation

Card offers `default`, `flat`, and `elevated` surface treatments. The variant changes border, shadow, and fine-pointer hover lift without owning the semantic element or the card's content. Hover transforms are absent for coarse or non-hover pointers and under reduced motion.

## Accessibility

<Callout type="info">
  Card provides presentation and anatomy only; semantic meaning comes from its element and content.
</Callout>

- All images must have descriptive `alt` text.
- Do not render an empty Card without meaningful content or an accessible purpose.
- Allow localized and long unbroken content in body and footer slots to wrap.
- Preserve a logical heading hierarchy inside card content.
- Keep keyboard focus on the actual interactive descendants rather than the visual container.
- Keep focus indicators visible when a composed control reaches a full-bleed card edge.
- Never rely on hover lift or media scaling to reveal content or actions.

## Visual Customization

Fourteen public roles cover the shared surface, border, radius, shadows, content inset, timing/curves and the three remaining geometry choices. Border width starts at 1px, upward Hover lift at 2px and optional media Hover scale at the unitless multiplier 1.03. Scale 1 disables enlargement without adding a semantic property. The transform token category groups that numeric visual role; it is not another source layer.

Flat remains stationary and shadowless but its optional image follows the same fine-pointer zoom treatment. Its transparent border retains a box-model width. Studio disables only the inapplicable lift control for Flat; both actual and simulated Hover respect fine-pointer and reduced-motion preferences. Content, typography within children and native actions remain consumer composition.

Studio exposes the existing media duration and both easing roles as well. The shared approved defaults and stable status from ADR 0290 are preserved; arbitrary custom values are not separately certified. Copy-and-own consumers must adopt updated CSS/tokens explicitly. Product Card keeps its independently accepted stationary shell and owns its separate image treatment; changing the base Card hover token does not override that contract.

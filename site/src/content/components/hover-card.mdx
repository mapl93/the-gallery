## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use hover card for short supplemental previews such as product, profile, or collection context.</Do>
  <Dont>Don't place required instructions, destructive actions, or long interactions only inside a hover card.</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Accessibility

<Callout type="tip">
  Hover Card appears on hover and focus-within. Use Popover when the content needs explicit open/close behavior.
</Callout>

- Ensure the trigger can receive keyboard focus.
- Keep the content supplemental and available through another path on touch-first targets.
- Do not trap focus inside a hover card.

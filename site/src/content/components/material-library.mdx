## Overview

Material Library presents target-owned material records as a responsive card grid with optional sample media, classifications, descriptions, and property tags.

## Presentation

The required `materials` slot owns the ordered cards. `title` and `subtitle` are optional. Each card requires body content and a material name; sample media, type, description, and property-tag composition are optional.

Material records, ordering, and data mapping belong to the target. The sample classifications and descriptions are fixture content, not a ceramics schema.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use concise material names and pair technical classifications with plain-language descriptions.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not treat the card anatomy as a fixed CMS record or use a visual sample as the only material identifier.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep repeated cards in a meaningful order.
- Compose canonical Tag when a target needs property labels.
- Use sample media only when it helps distinguish materials.

## Accessibility

- Use a heading level appropriate to the containing page.
- Keep material names and technical properties available as text.
- Give informative samples useful alternative text and mark decorative samples accordingly.
- Do not rely on sample color or texture alone to distinguish materials.

## Specification

**Dependency:** [Tag](/components/tag).

**Anatomy:** `.material-library`, optional header, title, and subtitle, required grid, and repeated `.material-card` records with optional swatch, required body and name, and optional type, description, properties, and property tags.

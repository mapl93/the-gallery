## Overview

Brand Story pairs target-owned media with a required narrative heading and body, plus optional eyebrow and signature content.

Media and narrative body are target-owned slots. Signature may contain a name, mark, or image supplied by the target; it does not imply authentication behavior.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Use specific, credible narrative content that connects the media and story.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not use Brand Story as a generic promotional banner or invent interactive behavior for the signature slot.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep the eyebrow short and let the body retain semantic paragraphs, lists, or quotations as needed.

## Accessibility

- Use a heading level that fits the surrounding page hierarchy.
- Give informative brand media useful alternative text and mark decorative media with an empty alternative.
- Preserve semantic structure inside the narrative body.
- If the signature is an image, give it alternative text only when the visible mark communicates information not already present nearby.

## Overview

Auth Forms is a narrow, labelled authentication-entry shell. It composes canonical Form, fields, Buttons, Links, and Dividers while the target owns credentials, validation responses, providers, requests, sessions, redirects, and access to account data.

## Presentation

The artwork uses a sign-in fixture, but the contract defines a neutral shell rather than a canonical login/register mode. `form`, `forgotAction`, `socialActions`, and `footer` are composition slots. Their fields, routes, method inventory, server validation, submission, redirects, and navigation remain target-owned.

`title` and a complete canonical `form` are required; invalid missing required content omits the whole renderer. The supporting `subtitle`, divider label, recovery action, alternative actions, and footer may be omitted independently.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Compose canonical Form, Field Wrapper, Input, Password Input, Button, Link, and Divider behavior.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not duplicate credential state, field validation, provider logic, or authentication policy in U1.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep the visible title aligned with the target flow being presented.
- Include password recovery where the authentication method supports it and use a real Link destination.
- Give alternative methods specific names and use `type="button"` so they cannot submit the credential form.
- Treat providers, generic server errors, pending/disabled policy, rate limits, MFA/passkeys, redirects, sessions, and submission as target integration concerns.
- Login and registration entry points may be public; access to resulting account data remains controlled by the target authentication system.
- In Shopify v1, hand off to the current managed customer-account authorization experience or an authenticated headless target; do not substitute a classic Liquid credential form. Classic customer accounts require a separately versioned compatibility adapter.

## Accessibility

- Label the section with its visible title; no special authentication ARIA role exists.
- Associate every field with a visible label and connect target validation messages with `aria-describedby`.
- Use correct native `type`, `name`, and `autocomplete` values; preserve paste, autofill, password managers, and the canonical reveal action.
- Give each alternative authentication action a method- or provider-specific accessible name.
- Keep password recovery and account-mode navigation keyboard reachable with visible focus.
- Let the target announce generic authentication errors without revealing account existence.
- Preserve native form submission semantics and appropriate autocomplete values.

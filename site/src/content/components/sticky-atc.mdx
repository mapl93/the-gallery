## Overview

Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar keeps essential product identity, price, and purchase action available when target logic decides the primary Product Form is no longer visible.

## Presentation

Sticky Add-to-Cart uses the ADR 0069 viewport-edge family. The bar is visible as the primary artwork, is contained at the preview's bottom edge, and does not inherit geometry from its hidden trigger.

`visible` defaults to `false` and composes `.sticky-atc--visible`. `title` and `actionLabel` are required; media, alternative text, and target-formatted price are optional. `actionDisabled` defaults to `false`.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Show the bar only when the primary purchase controls are no longer conveniently available.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not let the sticky surface cover checkout, consent, or system controls.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Use the same selected variant, quantity, and selling-plan state as Product Form.
- Keep the displayed price synchronized with target commerce state.
- Avoid showing two competing add-to-cart actions at the same viewport position.

## Accessibility

- Keep the purchase action available through the primary Product Form when the bar is hidden.
- Provide `imageAlt` when the optional image conveys information; hide redundant media from assistive technology otherwise.
- Preserve a concise product title and a clearly named action.
- Do not duplicate live announcements when the primary form and sticky bar reflect the same state.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences when targets animate visibility.

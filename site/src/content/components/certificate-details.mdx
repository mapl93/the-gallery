## Overview

Certificate Details presents target-owned certificate fields with an optional heading, icon, and QR media slot.

## Presentation

`details` is required. `icon`, `title`, and `qr` are optional. The detail slot owns repeated target-supplied label-value fields without prescribing their inventory or record schema.

QR media is only an optional target-owned slot. The component does not choose a destination, protocol, verification method, or authenticity guarantee.

## Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Show labels beside target-supplied values and include only claims the target can substantiate.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not treat the QR slot or certificate styling as proof of authenticity.</Dont>
</DoDont>

- Keep field ordering meaningful for the reader.
- Use target-formatted identifiers, dates, and names.
- Provide a textual destination or equivalent information when QR content is meaningful.

## Accessibility

- Keep certificate labels adjacent to their values in source order.
- Use description-list semantics for label-value fields.
- Hide decorative icons from assistive technology.
- Provide text equivalent to meaningful QR content.
- Do not make verification claims the target cannot support.

## Specification

**Dependencies:** None.

**Anatomy:** `.certificate-details`, optional icon and title, required grid with repeated fields containing required labels and values, and optional target-owned QR media.

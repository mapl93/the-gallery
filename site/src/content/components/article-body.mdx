## Overview

Article Body is the `.prose` container for target-owned semantic long-form content. It styles headings, paragraphs, lists, quotations, figures, code, tables, callouts, image compositions, and optional product embeds without replacing their native markup.

The default presentation uses `.prose`. The only additional public presentation property is `dropCap`, which adds `.prose--drop-cap` to the root. Optional rich compositions retain the canonical selectors documented below.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Supply real headings, lists, blockquotes, figures, code, and tables from the target content source.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not flatten rich content into generic containers or use the drop cap as a substitute for text.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Keep the article hierarchy shallow and readable. Product destinations, imagery, and formatted prices inside `.prose__product-embed` remain commerce-target content rather than Article Body properties.

## Accessibility

- Preserve a logical heading hierarchy and native semantics for lists, quotations, figures, code, and tables.
- Provide useful image alternatives and captions for informative media.
- Keep wide code and table content horizontally accessible without clipping the page.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus for inline links and linked product embeds.
- Keep the canonical readable line length and do not rely on the drop cap to communicate meaning.

Core anatomy: `.prose`, native `h2` through `h4`, `blockquote`, `figure`, `pre`, and `table`; `.pull-quote`, `.pull-quote__attribution`, `.callout`, and `.callout__icon`; `.prose__full-bleed`, `.prose__image-pair`, `.prose__image-grid`; and `.prose__product-embed`, `.prose__product-embed-info`, `.prose__product-embed-title`, and `.prose__product-embed-price`.

Dependencies: none. The contract remains `pilot`; all semantic rich content is supplied by the target as one required slot.

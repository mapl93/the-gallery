## Overview

Featured Collection presents a short, deliberately ordered set of canonical Product Cards beneath a required contextual heading and an optional collection destination. Product queries, sorting, limits, availability, pricing freshness, loading, errors, and spotlight selection policy remain target-owned.

`grid` is the default and composes the canonical Collection Grid layout. `carousel` maps to `.featured-collection--carousel` and composes the canonical Carousel track and items as a focusable native horizontal product list; it does not imply autoplay, looping, dots, or an independent controller. `spotlight` maps to `.featured-collection--spotlight` and gives the first supplied Product Card more parent-layout space on wide containers without changing Product Card internals or choosing the record.

## Usage Guidelines

<DoDont>
  <Do>Supply a meaningful section title and one or more complete Product Card compositions in the intended commercial order.</Do>
  <Dont>Do not make Featured Collection responsible for product fetching, sorting, pricing freshness, inventory, loading states, or Product Card actions.</Dont>
</DoDont>

Use the optional collection Link only when both a descriptive label and real destination exist. Use Collection Grid for full result pages and Product Slider when finite previous/next navigation is part of the experience.

## Accessibility

- Associate the thematic section and native product list with the visible heading.
- Preserve `ul`/`li` semantics and every Product Card image alternative, name, Price, action, focus, and status requirement.
- Keep the Carousel list keyboard-focusable and natively scrollable; do not add a second Arrow-key model or claim autoplay behavior.
- Preserve source order in every presentation. The target owns data changes and announces loading, empty, error, or result updates outside this passive section.
- Omit the component when its required title or product collection is empty; omit the optional Link unless both label and destination are present.
